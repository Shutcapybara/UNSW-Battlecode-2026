# Setup

`bots/pace-v01` = `chaewon-y04-probe` (yuna-v05 family: atlas + newborn fix + portal probe) + a pace
controller, the only new policy. Targets from the public corpus, **winner medians** per map class
(compact = W*H <= 625): compact u25 7 / u50 12 / u100 23, t25 17 / t50 30 / t100 58 / t250 86; open
u25 6 / u50 11 / u100 20, t25 15 / t50 27 / t100 50 / t250 116 (q25-q75 in /tmp/pace_targets.json).
The curve self-anchors at the observed round-0 material. The controller shifts existing host knobs
only: split_val +7*unit-deficit and opening-window +<=30 rounds when behind on units; split_val -3
when ahead on units but behind on total; pearl/bed/memory values x (1 + gain * total-deficit) and
approach-only 2-step pearl sprints (length <= 6, deficit > 0.25) when behind on total, with the gain
class-conditioned (open 1.2, compact 0.3). Survival constraints untouched. Team total = clamped EWMA
of sampled visible lengths x UNIT_COUNT (floor 2*units); units exact from the header.

Two build-time findings worth keeping: (a) a full forage boost (x1.2-1.7) on compact maps
**collapses the churn economy** — trophy seed-1 isolation: u100 33 -> 11, W -> L; the compact gain
had to be cut to 0.3; (b) each separate stdout write costs 2.5 M — ACT markers ride the reply write
(protocol.py `pending`), which is what keeps p99 under the gate.

# The three questions the prompt asked

**1. Pace attainment.** The pace arm did **not** attain the field's curve, and did not move
attainment relative to the host: paired medians du100 = +0.0 on both classes, dt250 = +0.0 compact /
+2 open; on-pace share at r100 fell (0.24 -> 0.21 compact, 0.52 -> 0.45 open). Against this pool the
deficit is **death-limited, not willingness-limited**: mid-game dragons sit at length 2-3 (below
split_min 4), so a split_val boost has nothing to act on, and pearl-value boosts move foraging
patterns without changing net growth (compact churn: ~33 newborn deaths per 100 births in every
arm). The single-fixture schooltime jump (t250 70 -> 166 vs sinbad) does not generalise; against
stronger pool members (fenrir, yuna, ouroboros-m01, gavroche) the paired deltas are -3 to -7 pp.

**2. What moves with pace (win ~ stage statistics, all 1080 games, L2 logistic, standardised).**
Leave-one-map-out AUC **0.877**. Coefficients: dt250 **+1.26**, dl400 **+0.87**, dh2h **-0.78**,
dwall -0.58, dbody +0.54, du25 +0.38, portal +0.31, sonar -0.27, du50 +0.20, du100 +0.16, newborn
-0.14, dself +0.13, splits +0.04. The two statistics that carry the prediction are the **total-length
margin at r250** and the **longest margin at r400** — the mid-game material curve, not unit counts at
any stage; and the largest negative is our own head-to-head death rate. (Local half of the A2
question; consistent with the live record where the r100 lead predicts because it propagates into
these margins.)

**3. What the constraints cost.** Removing the survival constraints (nolimit) costs **-0.200 win
share** while buying **no pace**: u100 -1, t250 -13 open vs host, deaths 2x (wall+self 24 vs 12 per
1k, newborn 42 vs 34 per 100 births), survival at r250 down 7-9 pp on both classes. The constraints
are pure profit locally. Note the prompt's absolute gates are not met by **any** arm including the
host (wall+self ~12/1k vs <= 5; newborn ~34/100 births vs <= 10) — that is the family's baseline,
not the controller's doing (pace is delta-neutral on both: 12.4 -> 12.5, 34.4 -> 33.3).

# Falsifier status

The hypothesis ("matching the field's production pace raises paired win share") is **not confirmed**:
win-share delta -0.017 overall (compact +0.052 is the only positive cell, survival-linked rather
than material-linked: pace survives to r250 on 81% of compact games vs the host's 77% with equal
medians of material). The formal falsifier ("pace attained with delta <= 0") did not fire because
its precondition failed: **pace was never attained** (21-45% of games on pace at r100, target >= 80%).
The probe is therefore inconclusive-negative about the field question but decisive about the
mechanism question: valuation-level production control cannot reproduce the field's curve against
band-class opponents.

# What v02 / S2 should take from this

1. The field's advantage is **survival-while-producing**, not production willingness. The r250-total
   and r400-longest margins carry the outcome; the lever that moves them is deaths (h2h above all:
   -0.78 coefficient, doubling in losses per the live record), i.e. contact survival and newborn
   protection, not more production pressure.
2. The compact +5 pp is real but material-neutral — find what it actually is (survival tail) before
   scaling it.
3. The tile-threshold map class is too coarse: Queen of Spades (875 tiles, 25 wide) is
   compact-shaped and the open forage gain is the wrong instrument there (-0.36).
4. Newborn churn (~34 deaths/100 births family-wide) is the concrete number that keeps dragons below
   split_min — the structural blocker between the host and the field curve.

# Runtime (metered probes, vs sinbad-v07, both toolkits)

# Director paragraph

I built pace-v01 as the chaewon-y04 host plus a pace controller targeted at the corpus winners'
material curve, and ran the full three-arm seeded panel (1080 games, zero faults). Pace alone moved
nothing overall: paired delta -0.017 (p = 0.56), compact +0.052 / open -0.062, and — the important
negative — the controller never attained the field's curve in the first place (on-pace at r100 in
21-45% of games, attainment deltas ~0 vs host): against band opponents the material deficit is
death-limited, so production-side valuation shifts have nothing to act on. The panel's model says
the outcome is carried by the r250 total and r400 longest margins (LOMO AUC 0.877) with own h2h
deaths as the strongest negative, and the constraints-off arm shows safety is worth +20 pp while
buying zero pace. Next I would build the thing the field actually has and we lack: produce-without-
dying — newborn protection and contact survival aimed squarely at h2h/churn deaths, with the r250-
total margin as the target metric, and drop production-pressure work.
