# pace-v01 findings

## Target curve

main checkout or the pace worktree; a copy was requested and was not received
during this run. Targets are therefore public-corpus medians, not live-field
estimates. I decoded 460 corpus replays (284 open, 176 compact) with
`public_replay_review.analyse`, zero-filling a side's material after its
elimination so that early deaths remain part of the field distribution.

## Panel

The seeded unswbc 1.2.2 panel covered 10 maps × 6 opponents × 3 seeds × both
sides × three arms (1,080 scheduled games). The opponent set was Fenrir v18,
Sinbad v07, Gavroche v32, Ouroboros m01, Yuna v05 and Chaewon y04; no `clone-*`
directories existed. Host and pace-nolimit each completed 360 fixtures. Pace
completed 360; host has six timed-out games (all Slithery Fight vs Ouroboros,
seeds 1–3, both sides), so outcome comparisons use 354 exact pairs. Pace's
Schooltime/Sinbad seed-1 rows on both sides were rerun after the empty-action
fallback repair and replace the original two rows. The panel otherwise predates
that repair. Raw rows and fixture metadata are in `runs/pace-v01/`.

The pace arm missed the target curve. Compact/open median units at r25 were
6/6 (targets 6/6), r50 6/8 (9/9), and r100 5/11 (13/15); q25 units at r25,
r50 and r100 were compact 3/3/2 and open 4/6/7. The share reaching the r100
target was 10.4% compact and 28.2% open, against the 80% falsification gate.
Median total length at r250 was 0 compact and 2 open (targets 0 and 54.5); q25
was 0 in both classes. The compact total target is zero because the zero-filled
public corpus median is zero after early eliminations, so it is not a useful
production success measure by itself.

Pace vs host scored −0.483 (5 better, 176 worse, 173 tied; exact-pair sign
test p=1.03×10⁻⁴⁵); open delta −0.510 and compact −0.444. Median paired
material deltas were −1 unit r25, −4 r50, −6.5 r100 and −35.5 total length
r250. Pace-nolimit vs host scored −0.514 (0 better, 182 worse, 172 tied;
p=3.26×10⁻⁵⁵), with class deltas −0.514 open and −0.514 compact. The direct
pace minus pace-nolimit delta was +0.028 (16 better, 6 worse, 338 tied;
p=0.0525), +0.069 compact and 0.000 open. Thus the survival guards cost no
measurable win share on this panel, but they did not rescue the strategy.

Median self+wall deaths were 6.264 per 1,000 dragon-turns for pace and 8.545
for pace-nolimit; the required ceiling is 5. Median newborn deaths within 10
rounds were 26.49 and 27.27 per 100 splits respectively; the required ceiling
is 10. Both hard survival targets failed. Pace markers appeared in 357/360
pace games and no-limit markers in 358/360.

The all-arm, all-opponent ridge logistic model had leave-one-map-out AUC 0.915
(10 folds), overall AUC 0.938. The two strongest standardized coefficients by
absolute value were splits per 100 turns (+2.998) and head-to-head deaths per
1,000 turns (−2.128); wall deaths were next (−1.916). These are predictive
associations, not causal estimates. Pace's added split pressure did not
increase material: its exact-pair r100 and r250 medians fell sharply vs host.

## Director note

Pace alone did not move wins in the intended direction: it missed the field
curve, lost paired material and score, and failed both survival ceilings. The
falsifier did not cleanly test the causal hypothesis because the r100 pace
attainment rate was below 80%. The next build should remove broad enemy-radius
and 16-cell child-space vetoes, keep exact dead-step filtering and free-child-
exit checks, and target a substantially higher r100 attainment before another
full panel. Add telemetry for why candidate splits are rejected.
