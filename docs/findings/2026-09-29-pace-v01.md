## 4. Why the controller does nothing
- **Production is pearl-bound, not decision-bound.** Units ≈ total/2.3 throughout the opening; the host already splits
  at L ≥ 4 into 2-segment children with split value 8. ACT:pace+ fires ~9 times per game before r100, but on the
  splits the host would have made anyway (Queen of Spades: identical unit curves with and without the push).
- **Locally the host is near the field's unit pace already** (vs sinbad-v07: u100 17.5–19; yuna-v05 21.5). The live
  deficit (9–14 at r100) therefore comes from live opponents killing or out-foraging our units, not from a local
  production shortfall. Local instruments cannot reproduce it with the current opponent set.
- **The hold is the only active part**, and a class target makes it wrong at the extremes: on Schooltime it cut u100
  31.5 → 22 and t250 125 → 72 (seed 1) because the class target (19) is below the map's field value (26). Compact
  t250 rises (38.5 → 41, l400 4 → 6) and compact win share +0.049; open falls −0.046. The per-map curve (v02) removes
  the bias but gains nothing.
- **Length lost to deaths is the real pace tax.** Host length lost by r150 (vs sinbad, seed 1): Slithery ~800,
  Portals ~350, Devil ~300; ~70 % of it is trapped deaths (0 free exits). On Slithery a salvage mill runs all game in
  two 1-wide, 3-bed dead ends at (16,7) and (23,7): a 4-long dragon enters, eats to 6, splits 4 out, the 2-head dies
  (≈ 770 newborn deaths in one game). Removing the pocket-farm discount (v03) raised compact u100 13 → 17 and compact
  on-pace 0.33 → 0.48 — the only arm that moved pace — and did **not** raise win share (compact −0.005, open −0.089).
  The mill is not farm-driven; it survives in v03.

## 5. What predicts winning locally (all 1,226 panel games, one row per game, L2 logistic, leave-one-map-out)
- ours − theirs, all 14 statistics: LOMO AUC 0.90. Largest coefficients: h2h deaths/1k −0.76, longest r400 +0.69,
  total r250 +0.50, sonar rays +0.40 (a proxy for dragon-turns alive), units r50 +0.37.
- **The two statistics that carry it: total r250 and longest r400** (0.88 as a pair; best pair longest r400 + sonar
  0.90). Single best: total r250 0.87. With own statistics z-scored within map: total r250 + h2h deaths 0.84.
- Early only (units r25/r50/r100, ours − theirs): AUC 0.83; units r100 alone 0.81 — the local echo of the live
  "r100 lead predicts the outcome" finding. It is a **relative** lead: own units z-scored by map give 0.73.
- Caveat: late statistics are partly outcome (an eliminated side has longest r400 = 0); units r100 is not.

## 7. For the director
Pace alone moved nothing, because the controller could not move pace: on a band host, local production is already
near the field's unit curve and is bounded by pearls, so raising the split value is inert, and holding production
when ahead helps compact maps a little (+0.05, p 0.25) and hurts open ones by the same amount. The one arm that did
raise pace (v03, no pocket farming: compact u100 13 → 17) lost win share, which is evidence against "pace is
sufficient" but not a clean falsification (attainment stayed below 80 %). Locally the win is predicted by *relative*
material — total r250 and longest r400, and units r100 as a lead over the opponent — and by h2h deaths, i.e. by what
happens between the two sides, not by our own production curve. What I would build next: (1) an opponent set that
reproduces the live deficit (the clones of 62/545) — without it no local pace experiment can see the problem;
(2) a mill/trap guard (no entry into 1-wide dead ends whose only exit is a salvage split), measured by length lost
before r150 rather than win share; (3) keep the controller only as a per-map hold on compact maps. Do not register
pace-v01 for screening (priority 0).
