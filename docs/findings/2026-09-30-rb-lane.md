## What the replays say about the parent

1. **Early economy is the weak checkpoint.** p@50 is 0.97 of the field median (percentile 0.46), rising to 1.38 at
   p@250. The deficits are on gated maps: trauma 0.17, portals 0.68, dilemma 0.71, default 0.72. On trauma and

   search values an unseen open cell (5) above an unpaired portal (3), so dragons sweep empty ground first.
2. **Churn is the dominant pearl flow.** About 257 own deaths per game, mostly length 2–3; about 66 % are doomed at
   the start of their turn (no free neighbour). About 31 % follow a split: the deliberate pocket farm (enter a
   dead end for its pearls, split the tail out, the length-2 head dies). About 44 per game are sealed by an ally's
   body in a small room.
3. **Every ally head-on collision is a blind portal crossing** (766 of 766). 7.7 % of our crossings kill two of our
   own dragons. 65 % are same-round exit arrivals (the exit occupant moved first).
4. **Losses.** 11 of 25 seed-1 losses are eliminations: many small dragons rammed head-on while the opponent grows.
   The rest are lost on the longest dragon: our total at round 380 is often equal or larger, but consolidation
   (feeders suicide, the crown recovers at most half) shrinks it and the final longest is 21–38 against 30–57.
   Changes acting only after round 250 cannot move an economy checkpoint under this gate.
5. **Pearls lie uneaten on access-limited maps:** a standing stock of about 90 on portals, 46–60 on trauma, 35–53
   on queen of spades. Access, not supply, limits those maps.

## What paid, what did not, and why

- **Nothing accepted yet.** The stack adds 0 against `aline-01-nodevil`.
- **Exploration through portals is the largest economy lever found** (04: +0.17 pool, +0.12 gen at s1), but raw
  exploration pressure converts into deaths: more blind landings, fewer dragons, shorter at r100, fewer wins.
  The economy metric counts corpse re-eats, so it can rise while strength falls; the length and unit guards are
  what catch it.
- **Priced conventions for portal traffic do not work on this bot** (05, 06). The information needed (the landing
  of an unpaired portal, a crosser behind an exit) is not available to either dragon, and the expected loss per
  step is below a pearl's value.
- **The pocket farm is net positive** (03). The deaths it causes are the price of the pearls it harvests.
