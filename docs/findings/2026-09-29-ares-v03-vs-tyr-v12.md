# Ares V03 vs Tyr V12 — pure C++ port screen

## Result

Tyr V12 beat Ares V03 **12–8** across ten live maps and both seats. Each map was played once with Ares as Team A and once as Team B. All 20 games returned successfully with no draws, runner errors, or Ares fallback logs. The benchmark feature extractor processed all 20 replays.

## Port changes

V02 retained Anna's lexicographic safety tiers and a hard room-valid split gate; it also applied an enemy-distance penalty and loaded map atlas knowledge. Those choices changed Tyr's scalar rankings and produced a 20–0 loss in the earlier direct screen.

V03 removes those action rules and the atlas lookup. It uses Tyr V12's scalar candidate scoring, cell-specific expected threat cost, bounded sprint candidates, scalar trap penalty, child-area split check plus parent-room penalty, opening and trapped-tail splits, and resource/momentum/Devil terms. Radio roles, food and density reports, and newborn separation are carried into C++. The C++ controller adapter and persistent world tracker still come from Anna; body reconstruction and remembered state are not guaranteed to match Tyr's Python runtime turn for turn.

Ares has 1.168 field-median births at r100 but only 0.820 field-median units and 0.818 field-median total length; its newborn losses are also higher. Its paired share of pearls is 0.504 at r100, but its paired total-length share is 0.451 at r100 and pearl share falls to 0.440 at r150. This points to weaker retention after splitting. The r250 raw counts include the effects of earlier eliminations and should not be read as a clean late-game comparison. The pearl-curve figures use map references across this selected roster; they do not override the head-to-head outcome or establish performance against a fixed panel. The split-selection policy is now Tyr's, so any remaining gap can also come from C++ world-state, timing, or runtime differences. This screen does not isolate those effects.

## Method and artifacts

- Bots: `ares-v03-tyr12-pure-cpp-port` and `tyr-v12-devil-scout-tiebreak`.
- Maps: Autarky, Default, Devil, Dilemma, Portals, Queen of Spades,
  Schooltime, Slithery Fight, Trauma, and Trophy.
- Fixtures: seed 1, one game per map from each seat (20 games).
- Runner: unswbc 1.2.2, native games.
- Build: `c++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic` completed without warnings.
- Extractor: 20/20 replay files processed; benchmark references were read from
  `docs/analysis/benchmarks/`.
- Runtime source fingerprint (C++/headers and `bot.toml`):
  `a2aa17f17501e7e2fd2722fc67e725fdb5fdcfdfe3f96c2acaf0a14cccec6369`.
- Logs, results, standings, manifest, extracted features, cache, and replays are
  in the ignored `build/ares-v03-vs-tyr-v12-20260929/` directory.

The 20-game direct screen is smaller than BENCHMARKS.md's 140-side-game fixed-panel run and does not pass or fail that acceptance gate. Ares V03 remains experimental. No policy changes or follow-up games were made after this screen.
