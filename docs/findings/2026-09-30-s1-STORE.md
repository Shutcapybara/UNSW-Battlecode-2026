# The s1 store

There are two stores with the same schema, and neither is committed:

- `build/s1/corpus/`: every public game with a current top-50 side (non-dev ladder order at the latest snapshot) or
  team 7. That is 39k games from 26–30 Sep.
- `build/s1/local/`: local panels (for example the renoir runs).

Each table is a folder of `part-*.parquet` files, one per committed batch of 200 games. A batch is committed when its
`sides` part lands, so a killed build loses at most one batch. Re-running resumes, and a game decoded twice keeps its
first copy.

```
python3 tools/s1/build.py games                  # corpus games + teams from index.jsonl + ladder/ (seconds)
python3 tools/s1/build.py corpus [--jobs N]       # decode new corpus games (Mac: .venv/bin/python, all cores)
python3 tools/s1/build.py local --glob 'build/ra/runs/*/*/replays/*.replay'
python3 tools/s1/build.py status
python3 tools/s1/q.py sql "select cohort, median(\"pearls@100\") from sides group by 1"
python3 tools/s1/q.py curve units --norm --maps pooled --out build/s1/plots/units.png
python3 tools/s1/q.py cols series                 # list a view's columns
python3 tools/s1/q.py norms                       # force a rebuild of the field medians / means / SDs
```

Add `--db local` to point the unprefixed views at the local store. `c_*` / `l_*` views always name one store explicitly.
Set `S1_FREEZE=1` to stop an automatic rebuild of the field norms while the store is growing (see Normalisation).

## Views

| view | unit | contents |
|---|---|---|
| `games` | game | index row, ratings of both teams at the latest ladder snapshot at or before the game start (`elo_a/b`, `rank_a/b`, `snap_lag_min`, `snap_before`), `elo_gap` = A − B, `result_a`, `ranked`, `autoscrim`, `map` (index name, PD layouts not split), `in_scope` |
| `teams` | team | ladder name, rank, current Elo, `crank` (rank among non-dev teams), `cohort`: top10 / r11_30 / r31_50 / us (team 7) / dev / other |
| `sides` | side-game | the full F1 feature-lab row (`docs/analysis/FEATURES.md`) + the s1 side extras below + `elo`, `opp_elo`, `gap`, `cohort`, `crank`, `name`, `opp_*`, `ranked` |
| `series` | side × round | rounds 0–150 every round, then every 5 to 500. After the game ends the terminal state is carried forward and `ended` = 1. State columns are values at the start of the round; `c_*` columns are cumulative event counts up to and including the round |
| `series_n` | side × round | every numeric series column ÷ the field median on the same map and round |
| `series_z` | side × round | every numeric series column as a field z-score, (x − field mean) / field SD, same map and round. Use this for low counts (deaths, transits) where the median is 0 and the ratio breaks |
| `series_d` | side × round | rounds divisible by 5; `d5_<col>` = change since 5 rounds earlier. This is the rate of change: `d5_units` is net dragons gained, `d5_c_eats` is pearls eaten in the last 5 rounds |
| `sides_n`, `sides_z` | side-game | side features ÷ the per-map field median, or as a per-map field z-score |
| `deaths` | death | F1 death row (class `cls`, length, age, `reach5`, `enclosed`, `near_portal`, `newborn`) + the s1 extras below |
| `transits` | portal transit | see below |
| `splits` | split | parent length before and after, child length, `child_died10` |

Every view carries `game`, `map`, `side`, `team`, `cohort`, `name`, `won`, so any split is a filter or a group-by.

`map` in the replay-derived tables is the decoded name. **`Prisoners Dilemma 10`** is the 10-dragon layout (same rule
as F1 benchmarks); `map_hash` identifies the orientation.

## Normalisation

- The **field** is every corpus side-game in the store, both sides. It is dominated by top-50 teams and their opponents.
- The per-(map, round) median, mean and SD are computed on a fixed hash sample of up to 1,500 games per map
  (`S1_NORM_GAMES`). They are cached in `norm_series.parquet` / `norm_sides.parquet` and rebuilt when the store grows by
  more than 50 % or on `q.py norms`.
- **The local store is normalised against the corpus field.**
- **Raw** gives the actual interpretation, **÷ median** gives relative scale, and **z** gives a robust relative position.

## s1 series columns (beyond F1's)

**Material and opponent**

- `units`, `total`, `longest`, `mean_len`, `top1_share` (F1).
- `opp_units`, `opp_total`, `opp_longest`, `opp_c_eats`, `opp_c_splits`, `opp_c_deaths`, `opp_territory`, `opp_seen_share`:
  the other side on the same row.

**Space**

- `seen_share` (F1).
- `territory`, `bed_territory`, `contested` (F1 BFS samples every 5 rounds, carried on rounds divisible by 5).
- `beds_seen_share`: beds inside some 7×7 view of ours so far.
- `beds_reached_share`: beds some head of ours has stood within Chebyshev 1 of.

**Travel from birth (Manhattan on the torus, portals ignored)**

- `disp_mean`: mean over living dragons of the distance from the head to its birth cell.
- `disp_per_turn`: mean over dragons of that distance ÷ age (age ≥ 1). This is the per-turn version that newborns do not
  muddy.
- `disp_max`: the furthest-travelled living dragon (noisy, easy to read).

**Swarm shape and density**

- `swarm_rg`: RMS torus distance of heads to their circular mean.
- `pair_dist_mean`: mean head-to-head distance.
- `nn_dist_mean`: mean distance to the nearest allied head.
- `clustered_share`: share of heads with an ally head within 3.
- `swarm_density`: dragons ÷ (π · rg²).

**Terrain and enclosure**

- `kelp_adj_mean`: kelp edges next to the head.
- `reach5_mean`, `reach_le8_share`, `reach_le15_share`: cells reachable in 5 steps with bodies blocking, on rounds
  divisible by 5. ≤ 8 is the L24 hazard band.

**Contact**

- `enemy_head_view_share`: share of our dragons with an enemy head in their 7×7 view.
- `enemy_head_dist_mean`, `enemy_head_dist_min`: nearest enemy head.

**Cumulative events (c_*)**

- F1 events: `c_eats`, `c_eats_bed`, `c_splits`, `c_deaths`, `c_death_<cls>`, `c_kills`, `c_rays`, `c_moves`, `c_sprints`,
  `c_dragon_turns`, …
- `c_steps` (cells moved), `c_idle` (no action or a 0-step move), `c_turnaround` (a U-turn over two consecutive moves).
- `c_transits`, `c_transit_blind`, `c_transit_double`, `c_transit_contested`, `c_transit_died3`, `c_transit_died_same`.
- `c_own_goals`.
- Deaths by context: `c_deaths_enclosed`, `c_deaths_newborn`, `c_deaths_near_portal`, `c_deaths_crowd`,
  `c_deaths_post_transit`.
- `c_sonar_recv`: rays that stopped on one of our dragons, excluding a sender's own ray. It splits into `_ally` / `_enemy`
  by sender; `_self` counts rays back into the sender's own body.

**Derived (cumulative ratios up to the round)**

`pearls_per_dt`, `bed_pearls_per_dt`, `corpse_pearl_share`, `steps_per_pearl`, `bed_capture` (bed eats ÷ bed spawns),
`births_per_dt`, `deaths_per1k`, `own_goals_per1k`, `own_goal_share`, `transits_per_dt`, `transit_died3_share`,
`transit_blind_share`, `transit_double_share`, `rays_per_dt`, `sonar_recv_per_dt`, `idle_share`, `turnaround_share`,
`units_share`, `total_share`, `pearls_share`, `material_gap_rel` = (ours − theirs) / max.

## s1 side-game extras

**Openings and leads**

- `first_enemy_head_view`: contact round. `first_fight`: the first enemy-credited death in the game.
- `gap10_round`: the first round the total-length gap exceeds 10 % of the larger side. `gap10_ahead`: 1 if that side was us.
- `lead@50/100/150`: ±1/0 on total length. `lead_flips_50_150`.

**Beds**

- `bed_first_share`: share of beds we reached first (ties count half).
- `bed_arrival_median`: median first-arrival round over the beds we reached.
- `beds_reached@50/100/150`.

**Portals**

`transits`, `transits@50/100/150`, `transit_pairs` (distinct portals used), `transit_surv3`, `transit_surv3_0_150`,
`transit_died_same_share`, `transit_blind_share`, `transit_double_share`, `transit_contested_share`,
`transit_double_died3_share`, `transit_blind_died3_share`.

**Births up to round 150**

`births_0_150`, `newborn_surv10_0_150`, `newborn_first_pearl_median_0_150` (rounds from birth to first eat),
`newborn_ever_ate_share_0_150`, `child_len_mean_0_150`, and `child_len_{1_2,3_3,4_5,6_up}_share_0_150`.

## Deaths extras

- `own_goal`: `cls` is one of wall / self / ally_body / h2h_ally / invalid.
- `ally_heads3` / `enemy_heads3`: other heads within Manhattan 3 at the start of the death round.
- `crowd`: at least 2 heads within 3.
- `transit_same_move`, `transit_within3`, `transit_blind`.
- `born`, `kelp_adj`.

## Transits

One row per portal crossing (a move step whose destination is not the geometric neighbour):

- `entry` / `exit` cell, `pair` (the two crossed edges, direction-free), `length`, `age`, `step` / `n_steps`.
- `blind`: the exit cell was outside all our 7×7 views at the start of the round.
- `double`: another of our dragons used the same pair within ±2 rounds. `contested`: an enemy did.
- `died_same_move`, `died_within3`, `died_within10`, `death_cause`, `death_round`.

## Known limits

- The view model is a square 7×7 around each head, ignoring walls and portals. It matches F1.
- Manhattan travel ignores portals, so a transit shows up as a jump.
- The portal pair identity assumes an exit edge adjacent to the exit cell in the travel direction. That holds for the
  ten ladder maps' same-orientation portals.
- Sides from the first ~3,500 corpus games lack a few late-added event columns. They read as zero, which is correct:
  a missing column means the event never happened in that game.
