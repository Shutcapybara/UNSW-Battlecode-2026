# Gustave lane report 1 (R-2c lane `rc`)

## The short version

1. **Sciel-03a's economy gain is not a density memory. It is a flat ×0.70 cut on unseen-cell and bed values.**
   - With 03a's constants (reference 0.5, weight 0.6, 5×5 kernel), 95.5 % of 1.54 M factor evaluations (instrumented
     replay, three maps) are exactly 0.70, 4.5 % are in 0.71–0.9, and none are above 1.
   - The decayed harvest density almost never leaves ~0, so the factor sits at 1 + 0.6·(0 − 0.5).
   - A memoryless control, the same code with the factor clamped to 0.70, reproduces 03a on every metric on the same
     160 fixtures: Δecon~ −0.003 [−0.020, +0.023], win 0.000, hygiene within ±4 %, and 92/160 fixtures with identical
     pearls@100.
   - This is Renoir's 07a/07c lever (lower exploration value → churn economy) under another name.
2. **The density term itself, isolated, is flat.** A bonus-only version (zero field = the parent exactly; weight 3 or
   6; with the ally-saturation discount on the bonus) scores +0.008 [−0.025, +0.035] and −0.005 [−0.029, +0.030].
   Hygiene is unchanged.
   - Sciel's proposed 03b fix (shrink the factor's departure from 1 near allies) would have *raised* values near
     allies, because the departure is downward. It would have increased crowding.
3. **Ally head-on deaths are portal landings, not contests.** All of our ally-h2h deaths in the 160-game flat-cut run
   are near a portal.
   - Of the 1,830 collisions with known geometry, 98 % are a dragon crossing a paired portal blind and landing on an
     ally head it cannot see.
   - That ally was on the exit mouth, not heading into the portal, in ~61 % of all collisions, and queueing to cross the
     other way in ~21 %.
   - A visible-contest rule (05a, asymmetric yield) moved the rate −3 to −5 %.
4. **Search width keyed on "last search found no food" loses:** −0.043 [−0.072, −0.005]. Lune's opening harm
   reappears at every phase. Also, Lune's "late cap ×8" is in effect 48 → 160, not 384: `min(search_cap = 160, 384)`.
5. **A structure-keyed bed guard (L11) is inert at the natural key.** Only 6 % of decisions see ≤ 2 known beds within
   8 cells (61 % see ≥ 11), so the pool barely changes (Schooltime only).

Nothing has passed the D-032 gate. The accepted stack is still `gustave-01-nodevil`.

## What the lane learned

**L12 has no positive evidence left.**
- The one mechanism that "cleared the economy bar" did so through the value scale, not the memory.
- The memory term, isolated, is flat at two weights.
- A per-unit harvest field is too sparse to steer anything. One dragon eats a handful of pearls in a 5×5 window per
  30 rounds, which is a density of ~0.05–0.2.
- A shared field (sonar gossip of eats) would be denser, but it is a new mechanism with its own cost. It does not
  follow from anything measured here.

**The exploration-value economy is real and robust, and its whole cost is one leak.**
- The flat cut is +0.087–0.090 on the pool (lb +0.044–0.050) in two independent builds.
- Its guard failures are the self-inflicted rates, dominated by ally head-on (+31–35 %).
- The anatomy locates that leak at portal mouths.
- If a mouth rule removes the ally head-on excess without an economy cost, the stack "mouth rule + value cut" is the
  first candidate in the programme with a realistic path through D-032. That is the lane's next step.

**Caution-by-count keeps losing; the lane is now testing "where", not "how much".**
- 05a changed who enters a contested cell, but targeted the wrong geometry.
- 07a changes where dragons stand (portal mouths), not how many transit. The difference from Sciel-02a is no re-transit
  brake and no mouth zone after our own transit, the two parts most likely to have cost 02a its units.

**Search width.** Wide search on a "nothing found" trigger is the opening failure mode everywhere. Sparsity is not the
selector, at least not with that trigger. If the lever exists, it is in what the search values (R-5), not where it
looks.
