## TL;DR

1. **Tempo is validated as a strength measure on a fixed panel.** Across 247 local bots, tempo correlates with
   Bradley–Terry strength at Spearman **−0.84** (fewer rounds behind = stronger). Live, against Elo, it is −0.40; that
   number is noisier because the opponent mix varies and decoy games are included.
2. **Three behaviours predict strength in both worlds, with the same sign.**

   | behaviour | local, vs panel strength | live, vs Elo |
   |---|---|---|
   | distance to enemy heads | **+0.79** | +0.47 |
   | enemy-contact share | **−0.82** | −0.46 |
   | top-1 length share (length concentrated in one dragon) | **−0.79** | −0.46 |

   These confirm the handoff (`2026-10-01-s1-handoff-niches.md`). Winning is avoiding enemy contact and spreading
   length across many dragons. Weaker but agreeing in both worlds:
   - displacement per turn;
   - births and transits by r50;
   - an earlier first pearl;
   - a lower death rate after transits.
3. **The strongest live niches have no local bots in them.** The niches holding most of the live top ten are 2, 10, 11
   and 9, together with niche 8 (tungtung67), which has none of the top ten. Between them they contain three local
   bots, all from the hb1 series.
4. **The best local bots form their own niche.** Niche 0 holds 60 bots, the best at 1774. Its bots are already
   contact-averse and spread, but **make fewer early transits and births than the live top teams**. No live team
   plays this way.
5. **Team 7 live sits in niche 13** on every day from 27 to 30 Sep. Its live neighbours are Computers and Just Keep
   Swimming (Elo ~1750–1850). Its profile is the most enemy contact, the least clustering, the fewest early transits
   and the fewest early births of any niche.

## Fit

- 30 behaviour features: field z-scores per map, then averaged over maps. Outcome-flavoured columns are excluded.
- PCA on the joint set; k = 14 chosen by silhouette.
- PC1 explains 28 % of the variance.
  - **+** births and displacement at r50, transits;
  - **−** contact, first pearl, nearest-ally distance, top-1 share.
  - It is the "production and tempo" axis, and correlates with strength in both worlds (local +0.68, live +0.45).
- PC2 (12 %) is mainly local: swarm radius and sonar against vision and clustering.

## Strong and novel

These are the local bots above the 60th-percentile strength floor (1540), ranked by novelty in PCA space:

1. **hb1-04-deployable** (1561, novelty 3.8) is the only local bot in the calc/Stockfish niche. It also has **the
   fastest opening of any local bot (tempo −10.7)**. Its strength stays mid-pack despite that, so the top-team opening
   is reproducible locally and something after the opening is losing it games.
2. **eunchae-s02-pearl-band** (1585, novelty 2.2) sits in the PPP niche.
3. **hb1-12** and **hb1-13** (1774 and 1721, novelty 2.1) lead niche 0.
4. **r3-02-exit-known** (1633, novelty 1.7) leads niche 7.

Novelty is weakly *negatively* related to strength overall (ρ −0.24). Most novel local bots are novel because they
are broken.

## Reading

**What separates us from the strong side of the map.** The best local family (niche 0) already does the things that
correlate with strength in both worlds: less contact and length spread over many dragons. It lacks the two things the
live top teams add:

- early production (births at r50);
- early portals.

The PPP / yuna niche has the early portals, and the 中国必须人能飞 / Cutlery niches have the production. **That is
the gap the next iterations should close: niche 0's contact discipline plus niche 1's early transits plus niche 2's
birth rate.** This agrees with the handoff's actionability list (items 5 and 6, and the portal exit check).

**Our live submissions are not our best local bots.** Team 7's live days map to niche 13 and to local neighbours
hb1-11, tew and ein-dog (BT ~1450–1550), not to niche 0. Promoting a niche-0 bot would by itself move the live
profile toward the strength direction. That move still needs the decision gate before submission.

## Caveats

- **Live units include unranked games.**
  - SSS 09-29/30 lands in niche 4 next to the fry bots, which is the decoy bot, not the rated one.
  - Cutlery's niche is partly decoy as well.
  - The fix is the authenticity filter in `2026-10-01-s1-F-fingerprinting.md`. Until then, read the live side of
    niches 4, 5 and 9 with care.
- **Local z-scores come from a panel of six local opponents.** Contact and enemy distance depend on who you play, so
  local and live positions are comparable in direction, not in exact location.
- **Correlations are across bots, not interventions.** Contact avoidance may be partly a consequence of strength:
  strong bots are not invaded.
- **Bradley–Terry strength from 60–120 games has an SE of ~30–40 points.** Neighbouring ranks are ties.
