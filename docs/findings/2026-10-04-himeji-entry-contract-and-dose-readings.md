## Prospective contract for Seoul and Rome

This is Himeji's recommended concrete specification. Kanazawa's historical query and counts remain intact. It separates the optimistic capacity feature from immediate legality, and is not a claim that the old query implements it.

Dose0 bypasses the feature and preserves parent parity.
2. **Legality first:** retain the parent's engine-aware legal-action checks, including its own body and actual tail/growth handling. Do not remove all visible tails to decide a move is legal. Opponent future actions remain uncertain. If no legal one-step move exists, keep the parent rescue/fallback behavior; choosing a largest-capacity illegal move is not a rescue.
3. **Candidate-specific projection:** for each otherwise legal step u→v, project the queen body and length for that candidate, including its growth/tail retention. Other known bodies stay at their currently known positions; do not substitute future `R[t+1]` information. Unknown growth/state must be flagged and use a declared optimistic branch, not future replay knowledge.
4. **One fixed optimistic feature:** define Cb as the number of cells reachable from v, including v, capped16, with u removed and known projected body cells blocked. To retain Kanazawa's proposed tail relaxation as a heuristic, remove one terminal tail cell from each length>1 body consistently for every candidate; this is explicitly *not* the legality mask. Unknown terrain stays passable and a search reaching an unbounded unknown frontier is assigned cap16 rather than inventing a closed wall at the observed boundary. This feature is optimistic and may miss traps.
5. **Same eligibility rule for every direction:** eligible if Cb≥k **or** the induced reachable-set-plus-u graph contains a simple cycle of length at least projected L+1. This cycle exception is an optimistic geometry heuristic, not proof of a body-legal orbit; reintroducing u does not certify it is presently traversable. Veto otherwise, with k0/4/8/16 and strict Cb<k. Evaluate alternatives with their own projected body/length and the same cycle predicate.
6. **Selection/fallback:** among eligible legal moves retain the parent's ranking. If all legal moves are vetoed, choose largest Cb with parent ranking as the tie-break. No new invalid command may be introduced by this veto; no-legal-step states use rule2. Record how often the fallback fires.
