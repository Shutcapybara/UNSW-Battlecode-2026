`aline-17` stacks two mechanisms, each measured alone first:

1. **Symmetry inference (`aline-13`).** Every map is x-mirror, y-mirror or 180°-rotation symmetric, and a pearl tile
   shares its countdown with its mirror. Each dragon keeps the three candidates alive until an observed edge kind
   or bed flag contradicts one, and counts matching kelp/portal edges and equal bed countdowns as confirmations.
   It decides at one survivor with 8 confirmations, or adopts a teammate's decision from a new sonar packet
   (type 8). Once decided, it mirrors observed terrain into unknown edges, mirrors bed countdowns, and pairs any
   half-known portal whose mirror belongs to a known pair (pair(a, b) ⇒ pair(m(a), m(b)); partners are *not* mirror
   images of each other). This is measured structure, not map identity: debug runs decided correctly on trauma,
   portals, default and schooltime, usually by round 7. Alone it passed the full pool gate (+0.038, win +0.054) and
   missed generalisation narrowly: wall +10.3 % and win lower bound −0.032.
2. **Ally right-of-way (`aline-07`, the user's suggestion).** A move that leaves a visible ally head within 2 with no
   free neighbour, when it had one, costs that ally's dragon value if the ally has right of way. The longer dragon
   has it; at equal length, greater x, then greater y. Every dragon runs the same program, so both sides compute
   the same answer without radio. Alone it cost pool economy (−0.020) but cut self and body deaths and raised
   generalisation wins (+0.060 [+0.022, +0.103]).

The stack cleared both of 13's misses: gen wall +10.3 % → +8.7 %, gen win lower bound −0.032 → −0.001.

## Which kinds of mechanism paid

- **Information paid; pressure did not.** The one accepted mechanism gives dragons knowledge they could not
  otherwise get (the unseen half's terrain, bed timings and portal pairs). Every raise in pressure to explore or
  farm (bed waiting, global dive value three times, cheaper or dearer farming, stronger spreading) either lost
  economy or bought it with dragons, length or wins.
- **Global evaluation weights sit on cliffs.** The farm discount at 0.15 is locally optimal in both directions
  (0.05: wall +25 %; 0.25: −0.074; 1.0: −0.068). Dive value is similar: +pearls, −dragons at 4 and at 5, with or
  without symmetry.
- **Conventions work where both dragons can see each other, and fail where they cannot.** The right-of-way rule
  (in view) cut self and body deaths. Both portal right-of-way rules (05, 06) were null, because the information
  they need (an unpaired portal's landing, a crosser behind an exit) is invisible to both dragons.
- **Pearls eaten is not strength on its own.** 04 dive5 was +0.169 on pool economy and still lost dragons, length
  and wins; part of the gain was corpse recycling. The length, dragon and win guards did the real work.
- **Ablations locate channels.** Within symmetry, bed timings carry the economy, terrain carries most of the win
  gain, and terrain also causes the one weakness: on dilemma, known kelp makes the planner avoid the 1-wide
  fountain corridors (fountain eats before r150: 50.9 → 32.6 per game; 60.2 without terrain mirroring).

## Method notes

- Screening: seed 1 of both panels first. Stop when pool Δecon ≤ 0, when the pool upper bound is below +0.02, or
  when units@100, length@100 or win has a point estimate below −0.03. Mechanisms that should change a countable
  event are checked on replays first (05 and 06 were rejected that way without a screen).
- A fixture-cluster bootstrap is reported beside the gate interval: without the sandbox, a seed changes only pearl
  spawning, and a few fixtures (3 of 160 pool, 8 of 232 gen) repeat identically across seeds.
- The shared host ran at load ~105 on 16 cores, so a full gate took ~1.5 h instead of ~25 min. The queue sat idle
  for ~3.5 h one afternoon while the shell permission check was failing.
