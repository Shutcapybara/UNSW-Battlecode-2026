## Reading (what the rules should target)

1. **Volume is not the leak — safety is.** Team 7 transits at field volume (49/game vs 47–56) but dies at
   28.2/100 vs 306's 12.5. Team 70 shows high volumes AND high absolute deaths still win (rank 2) — but their
   wall/self near-portal count is ~zero; deaths there are ally-body/h2h, i.e. contested-portal collisions, not
   kelp.
2. **The wall+self signature is ours (and the band's).** 623 wall+self near-portal deaths in 35 ranked games vs
   2 for team 306. Dying into kelp or one's own body within two rounds of a transit is exactly what exit-memory
   + exit-cell simulation should remove; it is the single biggest bucket (51 % of our near-portal deaths).
3. **Portals map is a transit spam disaster: 399 steps/game (5.5× team 306), 154 near-deaths/game, 332
   same-pair doubles in 5 games.** The parity rule (§2 no-double-transit) alone has a large target here.
4. **Same-pair double-use is real and measurable**: 548 friendly same-pair deaths in 35 games (15.7/game) vs
   306's 4.3/game with equal transit volume — consistent with the 1,005 friendly head-on initiations finding.
5. **Don't stop transiting**: our cleanest maps by rate (Trauma 7.5, Autarky 9.1, Default 12.5) are ones where we
   transit moderately and survive; the willingness must stay (sakura's −16 regression from portal avoidance is
   the recorded counter-example).
