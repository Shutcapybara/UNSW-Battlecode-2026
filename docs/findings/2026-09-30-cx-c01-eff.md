- **Built**: the material ledger over F1 feature parquets — `tools/analysis/c1c_ledger.py` (aggregator) +
  `tools/analysis/c1c_frames.py` (portal transits, first-pearl lag) → generated tables

- **Pairs / falsifier**: no bot yet (chassis C1-A not landed), so no better/same/worse; the falsifier check
  replaces it — 4/54 statistics outside the band IQR pooled (one mechanism: the sprint tax), 35/54 on Portals.
  **The falsifier did not fire.** Fixtures give the baseline the fixes will be gated on (above).
- **Sandbox**: not applicable — nothing built on the chassis today.
- **What failed / was deferred**: idle-turn and waiting-vs-travelling stats, contested/missed beds, and
  per-pearl portal transits for the *field* cohorts (replay-side parquets don't carry them; field frames were
  never cached). Transits and first-pearl lag were computed for our own games from the decode cache.
- **Next**: the fix list in rank order below, on `cx-a01-chassis` the moment it lands, each a `params.hpp`
  switch measured alone in exact pairs (map, side, seed) vs the same fixture panel, accepted only if it moves
  its own statistic by the predicted amount AND pearls-eaten-by-r100.

## Method

Same F1 extractor as C1-E (extend, don't fork); leak classes are computed on its per-death rows and **overlap
by design**: **newborn** = child died within 10 rounds of birth; **trapped** = enclosed at death (≤15 cells
reachable in 5 steps, bodies blocking; the trapped/mill class), suicide excluded; **portal** = died within 2
cells of a portal (the portal-exit class); **crowd23** = length ≤3 dying into own-side bodies (the crowding
class). Causes (wall/self/ally-body/h2h-enemy/invalid) are the disjoint F1 classes. Currency:
**length lost per 1k dragon-turns, rounds 0–99** (0–249 also in the JSON). Team-7 live pools ranked + dev
games — 60% of opponents are teams 62/45/241/752/75 — and the by-submission table below separates the
versions. Full per-map tables: `docs/analysis/C1-efficiency-ledger.md`.

## Portals, the disaster map, in one paragraph

Our live side on Portals: pearls/100dt 16.4 (top10 11.4), moves/pearl 6.0 (top10 8.8), first pearl r4 — the
opening economy is the best in the field. And lenlost/1k = 131.7 (top10 82.6, band 70.7) with every leak class
roughly doubled: trapped 125.9, newborn 81.0, portal 67.8, crowd23 49.7, wall 19.6, invalid 8.1. Death-len per
pearl eaten: 0.80 vs 0.66 — we lose four-fifths of everything we eat. Portal transit exposure is NOT the
difference (0.39 transits/pearl for us, 0.40 for local opponents — the field transits constantly); dying at
the exits is. This is the map where the whole contest is decided for us (C1-E: compact games settled by r100).

## Schooltime and Slithery

Schooltime: our eating rate already matches the top ten; what separates them is near-zero trapped losses
(2.7 vs our 24.9) — their 41-unit swarms rarely enclose themselves, ours do. Slithery: everyone churns; the
top ten lose 55 length/1k to trapped+wall while we lose 137 (trapped 111 + wall 26); kelp-wall deaths are a
named secondary leak there (band 8.9, we 25.8).

## The sprint tax (new, ours)

`len/pearl + sprint/pearl = 1` exactly, so the two conversion losses are separable. Pooled, team-7-live pays
0.023 length per pearl in sprint costs vs band median 0 (q3 0.013) — the only mechanism outside the band's
IQR in both windows, i.e. ~2.3% of everything we eat. By submission it grew monotonically: 9508 → 0.005,
10473 → 0.029, 10357 → 0.044. The newer economisers sprint harder for pearls. A cap or a
sprint-only-when-safe rule is the cheapest candidate fix on the list (one `params.hpp` switch).

## Frame-level addenda (our games only, from the decode cache)

- **Portal transits**: ~0.4–0.45 transits per pearl for every population (team-7 live 0.477, yuna local 0.447,
  local opponents 0.526; Portals-only 0.39–0.40). Exposure is field-normal; the leak is exit survival, not
  transit volume — C1-D's exit memory / probe ray is the right shape.
- **First-pearl lag**: median 5 rounds birth→first-eat for children in every population, ours included. The
  newborn leak is not "children find food slowly" — it is where they are born and what they walk into. A
  newborn-targeting fix should site the child's first bed at birth (C1-B arrival rounds), not speed the search.

## Fix list for the chassis (rank order; each one `params.hpp` switch, measured alone)

1. **Trapped/mill escape** (Portals +62.6, Slithery +23.2, Schooltime +21.2): enclosure probe each turn
   (C++ makes the reach-5 BFS ~free); when reach ≤ k, break the mill toward open space even at pearl cost.
   Accept if trapped len/1k drops ≥30% on Portals/Slithery pairs AND pearls r100 does not fall.
2. **Newborn siting** (Portals +47.7, Slithery +22.9): child's first bed chosen at split time for
   arrival-earliness and crowd-freedom (C1-B supplies arrival rounds); crowd23 is expected to fall with it
   (overlap). Accept if newborn len/1k drops ≥25% on Portals AND pearls r100 rises.
3. **Sprint discipline** (pooled, the only outside-IQR effort leak): sprint only when the target pearl's
   arrival margin exceeds the payment risk; cap sprints per dragon per k rounds. Accept if sprint/pearl
   halves AND pearls r100 does not fall.
4. **Portal-exit discipline** — owned by C1-D; the ledger supplies its targets (Portals portal 67.8 vs band
   43.7; invalid deaths 8.1/1k, the field-unique self-inflicted leak). Note my `portal` class is geographic
   (died within 2 cells of a portal); C1-D's `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-d01-portals.md` attributes deaths
   to the dragon's own portal step exactly — read theirs for mechanism, mine for field rank.
5. **Wall/kelp avoidance on Slithery** (wall 25.8 vs band 8.9): fold into the router's cost map (C1-B), not a
   separate switch.

Wall-death note for the zoo: yuna-v03-core's local wall rate (8.0/1k pooled) matches the F1 lab's finding for
the old zoo (8–9/1k) while kazuha/ouroboros run 0 — the reference bot never fixed kelp navigation.

## Caveats

- Team-7 live pools 5 submissions and 60% dev opponents (62/45/241/752/75); the by-submission table separates
  the versions but per-cell n is small for 10413/10357.
- The local panel is two bots; its pooled absolutes are panel-relative (see table note). Only exact-pair gaps
  are read.
- Leak classes overlap (a newborn can die trapped at a portal); the ranking currency is per-class length lost,
  so overlapping classes each carry their own full weight — do not sum them.
- The 0–249 window and per-map q1–q3 are in `game_stats/efficiency_ledger.json`; falsifier per-map counts:
  Portals 35/54, Dilemma 21, Slithery 16, Schooltime 14, Autarky 10, Default 10, Trophy 8, Trauma 3.
- No submission ids since 28 Sep (D-023) — same cohort caveat as C1-E.
