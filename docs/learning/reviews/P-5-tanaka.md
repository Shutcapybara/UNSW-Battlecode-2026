## Gate, direction semantics and Sugawara's amendments

**Bind G-parent**: paired difference in unweighted move-turn accuracy, P1 minus frozen HB-1 output, whole-series5th percentile>0,1,000/seed7/linear on the frozen clean cohort. Print absolute0.83, queen/nonqueen, map, teacher, classes, log loss and calibration/entropy. This estimates agreement with the fixed teacher population; it does not guarantee policy improvement. The panel remains the behavioral test. I do not regard a pass as nearly certain: independent-series/map transfer and oracle selection remain substantial unknowns.

The parent has three predicted classes F/R/L; B receives no probability from that classifier, but its deployed search contribution defaults to0 while other directions receive nonpositive log probabilities. Specify the diagnostic classifier comparison (HB-1 cannot correctly predict B), the explicit four-direction P1 scoring and the exact switch-off behavior. Do not call an argmax over the parent's search bonuses its classifier prediction: that would pick the unpenalized B spuriously. Comparing entropy across different class supports needs the B share and a common declared support/floor.

Agree with reporting full-decision flip rate and prior entropy, **reject the proposed <1% no-panel stop**. It is a binding stop mislabeled report-only and has no demonstrated relationship to win effect. Rare queen-critical decisions can change outcomes; a small marginal fraction can touch many games. Measure full parent decision changes on frozen parent states, with per-game/queen rates, not just prior-class argmax. A forced-path counterexample is enough: one critical changed move among1,000decisions is0.1%, yet could decide the game. Keep it descriptive. The already carded λ={0.5,1,2} is a reasonable seed1 dose screen if the Chair funds it; freeze the selection rule and gate one nominee on unused seeds, as D-053 did. Do not tune λ on confirmation labels.

Cost correction:3M×1,193float32 features occupy14.316GB(13.33GiB) for one dense matrix, before dataframe copies, LightGBM bins, new HB-1 features or other workers; the card's~7GB corresponds to int16, not float32. Data owns extraction timing and the shared lock. No throughput claim was independently measured here.
