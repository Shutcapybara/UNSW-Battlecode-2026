## Required amendments

1. **Narrow the information claim.** ΔAUC compares an eight-feature privileged representation with a particular16-scalar legal representation, with different intercept/scaling/regularization geometry. It combines information, compression, model specification, speaker selection and estimation. More features do not order model capacity or make the gap necessarily conservative. It is neither an identified “price of opponent information” nor a guaranteed upper bound on sonar-recoverable value. Mean logits over processes is an ensemble, not a model of the full union of their observations; it also supplies no such bound. Report these as concrete predictive contrasts. A controlled information ablation would require a separately specified comparison holding model/representation choices fixed, not a new arm smuggled into this card.
2. **Separate legal features from privileged speaker selection.** Each process's scalar vector is locally legal. But the analyst chooses the queen if alive, otherwise the lowest-id survivor using global replay knowledge. Within this selected sample, is_queen=0 therefore implies queen death; an arbitrary nonqueen process in deployment cannot infer that from its own flag. Two worlds can give it the same visible block/history while the remote queen is alive in one and dead in the other, yet only the latter selects it as speaker. This is selection information, not an illegal encoder field. Retain the diagnostic but explicitly restrict its interpretation. Adopt per-cell speaker proportions and queen-speaker-only comparisons; additionally report nonqueen-speaker strata and support counts. R5 must name the actual calling process and training distribution before calling this its deployable leaf. Mean-logit pooling stays training-time/report-only absent a communication protocol.
3. **Non-significance is not equivalence.** The card says that lower ΔAUC bounds≤0 in four cells imply R5 can use V-legal “without loss.” They do not: wide intervals can fail to establish an advantage while allowing a large loss. Keep the registered3-of-6 positive-lower-bound rule as a diagnostic event, but delete the no-loss/R5 inference. If a future noninferiority claim is desired, preregister a tolerable loss and require the upper bound on(V0b−V-legal) below that margin on its designated cells; do not add it after this result. Missing/one-class cells or insufficient valid paired draws mean INCOMPLETE, not zeros that satisfy the falsifier.

Do not silently let dataset.py's default train-only filter remove those frozen rows. Emit a cohort flow with game,series,side,checkpoint,speaker,block provenance and reason for each omission, applied to all compared models.
