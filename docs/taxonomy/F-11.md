# F-11 — Threat map and support counts

**Kind:** Feature · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L10 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

For each visible enemy head near us, the cells it can reach in 1–3 steps (with its length), plus the count of our size-matched allies near that enemy.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: all SIN/MC hosts (`tactics.threat_map`), ares-v03..v33 and V06 descendants (support count added in V06), tyr-v16/v19/v25..v34 (path enumeration with pearl-funded reach), ouroboros (reach ≤ min(3, len−1), p_strike by steps), kraken (3-step reservation for later-acting enemies; initiative scale), hydra-v11, leviathan (reach ignoring length), fafnir/skadi size-matched support, fermi x01/x02.
- Code / docs: `bots/ares-v06-expanded-search-support/policy.hpp:37-41`, `:869-906` (mark_danger), `:1043-1061` (threat_cost consumer); `bots/yuna-v00-control/tactics.py` threat_map; `bots/tyr-v34-calibrated-pearl-sprint/tactics.py` L151-184; `bots/kraken-v05-safety/main.py` build_danger L641; `bots/ouroboros-v13-ladder/safety.py` L11-99.

## What it computes
- Computation (V06): for every visible enemy head within Chebyshev 7 of our head, BFS through free cells to reach = clamp(enemy_len − 1, 1, 3); `danger[c]` and `threats[c]` = list of {steps, enemy id, enemy length}. `threat_support[id]` = visible ally heads with visible length ≥ enemy length within tdist 3 of that enemy head (size-matched, "Fafnir").
- CPU: bounded by the cheb-7 cut and reach ≤ 3; gavroche-v40 threat-off probe: peaks still 76.7–86.7 M — "threat-map construction alone does not explain the CPU problem". Tyr's path enumeration lacks a visited set (`n in path` only) — cost not measured.
- Consumers: [A-11](A-11.md) threat_cost and strike admission, [A-04](A-04.md) (−threat_cost in the move score), [A-06](A-06.md) (split: −1.0 if child head in danger; threat on the parent), [A-10](A-10.md) (dive pays ½ threat), [A-05](A-05.md) (sprints enumerated only when an enemy head is within cheb 4 — proximity, not the map), [A-02](A-02.md) (kraken hunter target from echo radar; ouroboros −3 in threat map).
- Evidence it matters: weight moves are small on Ares: renoir-17a/c/d threat ×0.5/×0.25/×0: +0.012/+0.016/+0.019 (all reject; 17d [−0.017, 0.041]); monoco ra-11 ×0.70 +0.0024. Support: fafnir f12 size-matched support gauntlet 140–42 (+6), released; fermi x01 changed 3/859 threatened turns (inert); V06 support is part of V06's +0.0133 econ (not isolated). Tyr pearl-funded reach (v25–v34) rejected (v33 146–178, v34 37–71), confounded by override drop.
- Flags: hard-coded cheb 7 cut and reach clamp 1..3. Threat probability is highest (0.9) when *we* are longer — the cost encodes material at risk, not head-on outcome probability (reading of the formula; undocumented). "p_short 0.1 assumes longer enemies never bother — false in a 20-unit melee" (newton §2).
