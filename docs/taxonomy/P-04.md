# P-04 — Crowding: ally head-on, ally-body, crowd23, target convergence

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L12, L29, L33, L23 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Our own dragons kill each other: head-on collisions between allies, deaths into allied bodies, small (length ≤3) dragons dying into own-side bodies, and several dragons converging on the same target; the ally head-on rate is the guard that has blocked every economy-positive valuation change on Ares.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: Tyr/yuna lineage and Ares (all lanes), team-7 live, kraken/avery/ouroboros-v01 (early C++/Python), S1 swarms.
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` tier 2; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md`; `origin/r/sciel:docs/findings/2026-09-30-sciel-lane.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-ra-lane.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-ares-v07-chae-won-components.md`.

## How it is measured
- Hygiene rates per 1k dragon-turns: `death_h2h_ally_per1k`, `death_ally_body_per1k` — [F-42](F-42.md); reported in the three-number form of [F-41](F-41.md) (absolute · excess over top-10 · field percentile).
- Ledger row crowd23: length lost/1k dt r0–99 by dragons of length ≤3 dying into own-side bodies — [F-43](F-43.md).
- Guard use: any tier-2 rate up >10% fails the gate ([F-49](F-49.md), D-032).

## Discrepancies
- BENCHMARKS treats ally deaths as hygiene to push down; replay-association studies find friendly-kill rates positively associated with winning (+0.5 to +1.2/SD; close games 69.6% vs 26.8%) — BENCHMARKS: "busy, winning swarms also bump into themselves more" (`experiment_data/replay_analysis_20260926*`).
- Tier-2 levels for the same V06 base differ by pipeline: V06 finding head-on 0.974 vs sciel-00 h2h_ally 2.41 (lane.py copy).
- HOLD effect size by host: chaewon-y05 friendly portal head-ons 19→6 on one fixture vs Ares V07 −13.2% with expected score −5 pts.
