## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: flat waits and global bed-value raises rejected on Ares C++ (production) and on Python; arrival-ready beds
  (bed_wait 0) accepted on Python and inherited by Ares.
- Why it stopped: Renoir — "the crude 'wait on a ripening bed' form loses on open maps ... while winning on QoS/Trauma —
  the *keying* is the question, not the guard" (L11). Spike — "do not retry a global v_bed change". Sakura — camping
  "made dragons idle". The R-6 four-arm guard ladder (visible / memory / density / shared) was never issued
  ("GLM 5.3 (prompt pending)", R-index).
- Revival condition: L11 (0.35) — "a guard keyed on structure (bed density within reach, contact distance) not a flat
  wait; revives above 0.5 if arm (c) beats (b) off-pool". The structure-keyed form is [S-28](S-28.md) (local hold).
  L26: bed pull without safety pricing raises deaths (atlas V08).
