**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L20, L04 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Scale the expected-loss threat cost (`threat_weight`) or the head-on strike admission (`attack_margin`) on the Tyr/Ares
evaluator, one knob per version.

## What it computes / does
- Ares threat cost = threat_weight · max over threats of p·max(1, V(ours) − 0.5·V(theirs)), p 0.9/0.7/0.1 by length
  comparison, ×0.8 if the enemy needs >1 step, ×max(0.25, 0.75^supporters). Strike admitted if gain ≥ attack_margin.
- Arms: 17a ×0.5, 17b ×1.5 (queued, not run), 17c ×0.25, 17d ×0 (threat off); 21a attack_margin 0.5 → 0; ra-11 ×0.70.
- Python: gavroche-v40 threat-off diagnostic build; yuna x28 bold (0.7) / x30 careful opening (1.5).
- Host: Ares C++ (production) for the Renoir/Monoco arms; Python for gavroche/yuna.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on the production host (Ares C++, pool seed 1; Monoco seeds 1+2). Renoir's monotone reading: lower
  threat raises economy slightly and costs win share; Monoco's trap-weight series is monotone the same way (d3 notes).
- Why it stopped: Renoir — "The +0.05 single-seed bar is out of reach for one-mechanism patches on a tuned Tyr
  descendant"; seat-A 80-game screens "overstate by ~half" (17d: +0.044 screen → +0.019 full). Lane `ra` closed by
  D-034. L20 → 0.1 after "the second independent rejection on the right host" (Monoco).
- Not a wrong-host result: measured on the production line. What is unfinished is the gate: D-032 ordered Renoir's
  17a/17d/18a/18c "re-scored under it before anything else runs"; no re-score file was found (d1 notes).
- Revival condition: L20 — "revives only through the D-032 interval gate … or if a knob shows a consistent
  seat-independent +0.03"; L04 (0.6) lists threat among the weights with non-zero slope for a joint fit (R-5 → SF-1,
  not delivered).
