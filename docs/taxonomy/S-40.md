# S-40 — Local fight rules (refuse far from beds; converge-or-refuse) — never built

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** never measured · **Ledger:** L10 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Two cheap per-dragon rules proposed from the fight-anatomy study: do not initiate contact when the nearest observed
bed is more than 6 cells away, and do not enter a group contact without a second own head in the window.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: none — no bot implements either rule (grep of the ra, sciel, monoco, esquie lane tables; d1 notes).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/C2-fight-anatomy.md` (readings 5 and 8); `docs/findings/2026-09-28-director-decisions.md`
  D-030 (3); `docs/hub/HYPOTHESES.md` L10; project `claude/c2-0-status.md`.

## What it computes / does
- Proposed rule 1 (refuse far from beds): skip head-on/strike candidates when the nearest observed bed is > 6 cells.
  Expected sign (D-030): "kill-against and own deaths per fight down, economy flat".
- Proposed rule 2 (converge-or-refuse): no group contact unless a second own head is inside the contact window.
  Expected: "length lost per fight down".
- Intended host: Ares (D-030 overrides GLM's note that they would go "on the C1-A chassis": "everything is on Ares").

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: never measured.
- Why it stopped: not stated in source. D-030 routed both rules "to the R-2 open lanes as candidate mechanisms"; the
  lanes that ran (Renoir, Sciel, Monoco, Esquie) chose other mechanisms and no report mentions either rule — (inferred)
  untried because lane selection went to the economy/leak rows with higher weights (L12 0.7, L24 0.5) first.
- Revival condition: L10 (0.4) — "`ra`/`rg` mechanism runs; expected sign on deaths per fight, economy flat".
  Esquie names the gen fight-loss cluster as "the cluster where fight pricing, not navigation, pays"
  (`origin/r/esquie:docs/findings/2026-10-01-esquie-map-anatomy.md`:156). L33's fight→fight alignment term is the
  coordinated generalisation.
- Host flag: the evidence is corpus-level; the intended host is the production line.

## Discrepancies
- D-030 rejects "never trade when behind" (the top ten initiate more than the band when behind, 50.2 % vs 45.0 %) while
  GLM's C2 status proposed it among three rules; only the two rules above were routed.
