## What it computes / does
- register → metered preflight (Schooltime A, Portals B, + Slithery A, Trauma B after D-017; max < 80 M, p99 < 60 M) →
  upload `-ai` → 20 dev games → screen → confirmation → promotion with probation.
- v2 screen: 3 opponents × 10 pairs (D-015: 3 × 20 = 60 exact pairs), futility net ≤ −4; screen panel `[545, 752, 45]`
  on the dev allowance (D-026).
- v2 confirmation 12 × 10 pairs, efficacy mean ≥ +0.03, p ≤ 0.025, no map ≤ −0.25; v2.1: 6 blocks × 20 pairs, ≥ 4/6
  positive, interims at blocks 3 and 5.
