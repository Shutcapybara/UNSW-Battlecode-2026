# P-06 — Own-body / self deaths

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** — (guard in D-032; touched by L30, L26) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

A dragon dies by moving into its own body; part of the rate is deliberate feeding (a sacrificial step next to the crown), part is boxing salvage and swarm density, and it is the tier-2 guard that r3-03 and esquie-04 failed.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: zoo (six of eight bots), Tyr/yuna/Ares lineage, S1 swarms on portal maps (kazuha, sakura), ouroboros-s02 host (newborn neck case).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` tier 2; `docs/public-replay-review-2026-09-25.md` §1; `bots/ares-v06-expanded-search-support/policy.hpp:1287-1296` (feeder picks a dead move beside the crown — c1 notes); `docs/findings/2026-09-29-ouroboros-s02-portal.md`.

## How it is measured
- `death_self_per1k` — own-body collisions per 1k dragon-turns — [F-42](F-42.md), three-number form per [F-41](F-41.md) (absolute · excess over top-10 · field percentile). Top-10 median is 0, so ratio-to-top is undefined; excess and percentile are used.

## Status and open questions
- Open, as a guard. Whether the zoo rate is a defect depends on feeding style: public review and team-7 recon read most late self-deaths as deliberate feeding.
- Undocumented behaviour in V06: feeder chooses a dead move next to the visible crown (policy.hpp:1287-1296); no finding describes its contribution to the rate.

## Discrepancies
- Same bot, different levels: V06 own-body 3.662 (V06 finding) vs sciel-00 5.74 vs Lune L0 "self" 6.64 (Lune's arena does not split ally/enemy body) — pipeline differences (d3 Contradictions 1–2).
- ouroboros-m01 invalid deaths 10.9/1k "a bug" (BENCHMARKS) vs deliberate `SPLIT 1` imitation of Vibing++ (306 claude recon) — relevant because such deaths can be tallied as self or invalid depending on pipeline.
