# S-57 — Target hysteresis and momentum tuning (ra-06; yuna momentum; S1 hysteresis)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L13, L31, L32 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Make a dragon keep its current target or direction — a target-stickiness margin (hysteresis), an EWMA bonus for
repeating recent first steps (momentum), revisit penalties — to cut oscillation and commit through transits.

## What it computes / does
- Hysteresis: keep the previous target if prev × hyst ≥ best (Ares 1.25; ra-06 → 1.75).
- Momentum: MOM[d] = k·MOM[d] + (1−k)·[d = chosen]; bonus mom_w·MOM[d] on moves (yuna 0.6/0.6; x15 1.2, x40 0.9, x23
  decay 0.8, x39 off near enemies, x14 opening only). ein-dog w_dir 0.8 all maps (x04), off on ≤256-tile maps (x08/v02),
  room-gated (x15).
- Revisit: w_visit·visits (Ares 0.15; renoir 18a 0.05, 18c 0).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on the production host for the one Ares arm (ra-06); Python momentum is in the inherited host at the
  plateau value; no Ares momentum variation exists.
- Why it stopped: ra-06 — "all r100 material and the economy curve fell; outside resolution and fails guards, so no
  seed 2 or generalisation run" (monoco-status); ein-dog — "no tested observable (kelp fraction, bed fraction,
  renewable-bed share, size) separates the two map classes without overfitting"; Q2 — "Stop re-testing … whole-policy
  transfers whose local signature is 'fewer deaths' (9639, 9980)"; ouroboros-v03 "Kept only as a record"; L13 —
  momentum "not yet varied on Ares", R-5 (which was to read it) folded into SF-1, not delivered.
- Host flag: the momentum evidence is Python-host; on Ares only hysteresis (ra-06) and revisit (18a/18c) were varied.
- Revival condition: L13 (0.4) "R-5 sensitivity on the momentum weights; `ra` sweep; portal-step death rate … test
  them together [with L31]: momentum is a soft mode"; L31 (0.5) hard modes with hysteresis; L32 (0.4) belief-vector
  modes whose stickiness replaces hysteresis. sciel-02a's decayed post-transit steering is the one two-state form
  measured on Ares (portal leak −21 %, economy paid — [S-18](S-18.md)).
