# P-05 — Wall / kelp deaths

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** — (touched by L24, L05) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Dragons die by moving into kelp or a wall; in several lines most such deaths are forced fallbacks (no empty ordinary exit, no eligible split) rather than routing errors.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: six of eight zoo bots (kazuha-s01 and ouroboros-m01 at zero), Tyr/yuna/Ares lineage, anna-a02 chassis (boxed-in 2-seg children), kraken-v04, gavroche sandbox series (Trauma).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` tier 2; `docs/analysis/F1-status.md` §3; `docs/analysis/C1-pace-targets.md` Reading 5, 9; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-f01-leaks.md` §F-4; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md` (r3-04).

## How it is measured
- `death_wall_per1k` — deaths into kelp/wall per 1k dragon-turns, a disjoint F1 cause class — [F-42](F-42.md); three-number form per [F-41](F-41.md). The percentile has a ceiling: 30–50% of field sides have zero kelp deaths, so a perfect bot scores ~0.75–0.85 (BENCHMARKS).
- R-3 also reports wall as length lost per 1k dt ([F-43](F-43.md) cause column).
