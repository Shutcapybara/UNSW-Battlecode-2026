# S-08 — Trapped-split grafts and exit guards (Python)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L24 (not cited) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Python-era attempts to stop trapped deaths either by converting a certain-death move into `SPLIT L−2` (Vibing++
trapped-salvage rule as a stdout graft) or by penalising moves that leave zero or one onward exit (exit guard).

## What it computes / does
- t01 graft: when the host's MOVE is a certain-death first step, no enemy-head trade is available, L ≥ 4 and below
  the unit cap → `SPLIT L−2`. Frozen gate: activation ≥50 %, net ≥ +6, no map ≤ −2.
- scholze-v04: `w_exit_zero` 300, `w_exit_one` 2.0 (not while eating).
- skadi-v03: zero-exit penalty unless reachable pearls fund a net-productive escape split (24-node child-room budget);
  v09 softer (80-pt zero-exit, 1-pt one-exit).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on Python hosts (gavroche-v32, fafnir, gavroche-v54); none measured on Ares.
- Why it stopped: t01 — local bots "already split on 87–90 % of trapped L≥4 turns (fry 100 %)" so the graft had little
  to change; "Keep it as a documented null; do not retry without a changed mechanism". Skadi — "do not carry its v01
  penalty unchanged into the next candidate"; line then stopped "following the request to stop after this
  iteration" (skadi v13). Scholze — not stated beyond the component verdict (report ends at promotion).
- Revival condition: host mismatch is real — these ran on the Python lineages under the 100 M CPU wall with 36–108-game
  screens (σ ≈ 4.6 wins per 90 games, ouroboros README). The Ares-side forms are [S-03](S-03.md)/[S-05](S-05.md). No
  ledger row names these grafts.
