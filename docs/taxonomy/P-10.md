# P-10 — Economy and retention gap to the field

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L05, L17, L21, L12, L20, L04 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

We eat less than field winners at every checkpoint, and our best local bots that do eat like winners fail to keep it as length and units; 85–95% of our live losses already trail on total length at r100.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: whole zoo; team-7 live submissions (9508, 8540, 10013, 10473 …); Ares V04–V06 and lanes; anna/cx chassis (0.44–0.47 of field).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` §Summary, §benchmark set; `docs/analysis/C1-pace-targets.md` Readings 1, 2, 10; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md`; `docs/analysis/ATLAS.md` Q1; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r1-search-ladder.md` §Step 0.

## How it is measured
- Economy curve: pearls@{50,100,150,250} ÷ field per-map median, mean of four = `econ~` — [F-40](F-40.md); three-number form (÷ field median / ÷ top-10 median / field percentile) — [F-41](F-41.md).
- Retention: units@100|map, total@100|map (length) beside pearls — [F-42](F-42.md) tier 1.
- Efficiency: pearls per 100 dragon-turns and moves per pearl by cohort — [F-43](F-43.md), [F-47](F-47.md).
