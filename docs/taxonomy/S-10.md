# S-10 — Newborn separation / escape tuning

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** hold · **Ledger:** — (L30 names newborn churn as the pay-down) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

## What it computes / does
- Tyr separation: on a split into a corridor-looking spawn (degree ≤2 and area ≤11, or branches ≤1) the parent sends
  T_SPLIT on the rear ray; the child BFSes from origin (18/180) and head (12/120), picks a waypoint by area/branches/
  exits/away/resource, action bonus decays 0.91^age, ends at age 18 or ≥5 from origin in open area (27 `escape_*` keys).
- Yuna newborn exit: child with parent head within 3 ignores targets within 3 of the parent for 6 turns, push 1.0;
  `nb_mode 2` only if flood room < 12.
- ra-09: escape trigger area 11→15. yeji young caps: first 2 turns young_bfs_cap 90 / young_rbfs_cap 120.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: hold. On Ares (production) the only tuning (ra-09) is a hygiene-only hold committed OFF; the separation
  code itself is inherited and its exceptions fixed (V05). Python variants: accept (yuna v05, fenrir) on their hosts.
- Why it stopped: Monoco — "hygiene-only signals ... switches are off pending an accepted economy base to stack them
  on". Newton — "the lever must be positional, not valuation" (siting by enemy-head distance recommended, not built).
  Yuna — "Map-dependent: helps Portals/QoS, destroys crossroads". Kazuha (S1) — "The child's problem is crowding, not
  information".
- Revival condition: stack ra-09 on an accepted economy base (Monoco); L30 names newborn churn from cramped splits as
  the row to pay down. Newborn siting ([S-12](S-12.md)) was the positional form and was inert on Ares.

## Discrepancies
- Tyr separation exceptions fired "every game" in the live control fenrir-v18 (Slithery 300/game, Devil 35/1k own
  turns) and silently fell back in all Tyr versions — Python newborn results carry that defect (ouroboros-s02 finding).
