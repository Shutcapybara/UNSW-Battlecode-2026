- Verdict: reject on Ares C++ (production host), replicated by two independent lanes.
- Why it stopped: Renoir — "The +0.05 single-seed bar is out of reach for one-mechanism patches on a tuned Tyr
  descendant"; the cautious direction costs economy "one for one with the deaths it saves" (BASELINES §Avoid: "Any
  single Tyr parameter moved in the cautious direction (room, trap penalty, farming, child room)"). Monoco — lists a
  larger child-room gate among changes that "regressed the economy/material curve or failed to move their intended
  statistic enough".
- Revival condition: L20 (0.1 dormant) — "revives only through the D-032 interval gate" or as part of a joint move
  (R-5/SF-1 tuner). No trigger names child_area specifically.
