# S-11 — Birth certificate and neck fix

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L18 (S1 generation) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Give a newborn information at birth: a 64-bit certificate packet on the backward ray at every split (role, phase,
target, crown id/len, parent), and correct the child's own-body reconstruction (children keep parent facings, so
facing-chain reconstruction thinks the neck is free).

## Where it lives
- Lineages (all Python, not production):
  - S1 generation on ouroboros-v10-beacon: kazuha-s01, sakura-s01, chaewon-s01 (cert v2), yeji-s01 (+`cert_direct`
    s02–s06), gpt-eunchae (incomplete payload).
  - Neck fix: chaewon-s01/y-series (adjacency link), sakura-s02 `sk_neck`; ouroboros-s02-portal `infer_body` (neck =
    dest(HEAD)[FACE+2], extends chain across a portal); sinbad e23 newborn body-chain fix (→ serre-v01).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-28-{kazuha,sakura,chaewon}-s01-swarm-dissolve.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-28-director-S1-evaluation.md`;
  `docs/findings/2026-09-29-ouroboros-s02-portal.md`; `bots/yeji-s01*/README.md`.

## What it computes / does
- Certificate layout (sakura): tag+chk 12 | ver 4 | role 3 | phase 2 | target 12 | crown-id 16 | crown-len 7 |
  parent 8; child reads on its first turn. `cert_direct` also sends it on any straight ray that hits the child.
- Neck fix: link child segments by adjacency, not facing; s02 `infer_body` extends a partial own chain behind the head
  and across the portal the newborn's rear passes.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: certificate rejected as an outcome lever on the Python v10 host ("delivery works but moves nothing" —
  director S1 evaluation); neck/body fix accepted as a bug fix, never measured alone.
- Why it stopped: kazuha — "The child's problem is crowding, not information"; S2 anti-goal — "no certificate work this
  generation" (sakura-s02 README); S1 closed on the v10 host (D-016).
- Revival condition: L18 (0.15) revives S1 messaging only "as a late consumer". Host mismatch: never tried on Ares, which
  already carries a split handoff packet (type 7) and a route handoff (type 9, [S-14](S-14.md)). sciel-01a names "hand
  the child its first target in the birth packet" as an untested variant ([S-12](S-12.md)).

## Discrepancies
- Delivery rate: kazuha "every child" vs chaewon 35–49 % vs yeji 37 %; director: "the three numbers cannot all be right
  and the empirical check ... is owed" (never run).
