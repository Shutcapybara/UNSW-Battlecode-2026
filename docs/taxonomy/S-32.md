# S-32 — Late-only search cap (lune-r1-07/08)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** hold · **Ledger:** L01, L02, L03 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Widen Ares's target-search node cap only after a round boundary (r40), leaving the opening search unchanged, because
the R-1 depth ladder showed wide search hurts the opening and helps the late game.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: R-1 lane (Opus, "Lune"): lune-r1-07-latecap8x-only, lune-r1-08 (late cap whole-map); lune-r1-07 then
  became the base of every R lane from D-033 (as `<lane>-01-nodevil`, e.g. esquie-01-nodevil).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r1-search-ladder.md` §Summary, §Addendum; `bots/lune-r1-07-latecap8x-only/`
  (params.hpp `search_cap_late`, `search_cap_sparse`, `search_cap_late_from` = 40); Ares V06 phase guards
  `bots/ares-v06-expanded-search-support/policy.hpp:1145-1148`.

## What it computes / does
- Base: lune-r1-00-base (= Ares V06, atlas off). V06 target cap is 160 normally, 64 saturated, 60 for newborns, and
  drops to 48 from round 40 (`search_cap_late`), 64 sparse (`search_cap_sparse`).
- **lune-r1-07**: `search_cap_late` 48 → 384 and `search_cap_sparse` 64 → 512; opening (r < 40) unchanged.
- **lune-r1-08**: late cap raised over the whole map; ends identical to r1-07 because "the value-bound prune stops the
  late search before 384 cells in practice".
- Context: the full ladder (×2/×4/×8 on every cap, [S-31](S-31.md)) cost −0.04 to −0.11 expected score, all of it in the
  first 40 rounds ("dragons converge on the same distant pearl"); p@250 rose +0.11–0.13 at every level.
- Host: Ares C++ — the current production line. CPU: L0 p99 7.54 M / max 8.52 M; all caps unbounded max 22.42 M.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: hold on Ares C++ (production host), z1 seeds 1+2 and gen panel — "does not formally pass the local gate";
  "ready for `register.json` as a dev-screen candidate … It is not registered here" (r1 finding).
- Why it stopped: handed on, not rejected. The finding names the next measurement for R-5: "the small end (96 and 192)
  and the phase boundary". D-034 registered it for the dev screen at priority 510, but the executor had been in
  shadow since D-031, so it was never screened live (inferred from D-031/D-034). R-5 was "folded into SF-1" and nothing
  was delivered; the D-032-mandated re-score of "Lune's late-cap level" was not found in the corpus (d1 notes).
- It did not stop as a base: D-033 made lune-r1-07 (with the 32×16 terms off) every lane's parent.
- Revival condition: L02 (0.7) — "cap by observed local pearl sparsity, not round"; L03 — composition check at matched
  r50 state; the round-40 boundary is a hard round threshold, which the lead's standing note ("key it on measured
  state") and the H10 falsifier treat as the weaker form.

## Discrepancies
- The r1 finding says the candidate "is not registered here"; D-034 registers it (priority 510). Both are true in
  sequence; the live screen never ran.
- The V06 finding's "+12.9 % dragons at r100" (L01 evidence) "does not reproduce on the pool with this scorecard
  (+0.011 at seed 1, −0.025 at seed 2)" (r1 finding §Step 0), so the base R-1 improves on is itself scorecard-dependent.
