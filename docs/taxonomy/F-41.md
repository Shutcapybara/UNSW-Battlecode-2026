# F-41 — Three-number form and field references (field_references.json)

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** — · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Reporting convention: every benchmark as (absolute value, gap to the top-ten median, field percentile on the same map), backed by per-map field reference quantiles.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: analysis (F1 / BENCHMARKS); cited by cx-f status, cx-f01-leaks, r1-search-ladder, ares-v01 finding, esquie brick list ([F-48](F-48.md)), T-1 P entries.
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` §"Relative to the field: average, top and rank"; `docs/analysis/benchmarks/field_references.json` (per map: median, p10, p25, p75, p90, top10, n — 12 metrics: pearls@50/100/150/250, units@100, total@100, births@100, avoidable_deaths_per1k, death_wall/self/ally_body/h2h_ally_per1k); `field_distributions.json` (referenced, not present).

## What it computes
- Computation: absolute; gap to top-ten (ratio for economy, excess per 1k for death rates); percentile within the field's per-map distribution. "Optimise on percentile".
- CPU: offline.
- Consumers: [A-27](A-27.md) (reporting and gate context; three-number rows in P entries). No bot consumer.
- Evidence it matters (as a yardstick): pearls@100 percentile map share 0.22 vs 0.45 for ÷median; kelp-death percentile resolves a change in ~13 side-games vs ~20 absolute; cross-map correlation 0.76 vs 0.60. Ceiling for zero-inflated rates: "a perfect bot scores about 0.75–0.85, not 1" (30–50 % of field sides have zero kelp deaths). Ratio-to-top fails for deaths (top-10 median = 0 for four of five death rates).
- Flags: top10 values sometimes below the median (Portals pearls@100 median 133.5, top10 128; PD10 58.5 vs 50) — top-10 teams are not always the heaviest eaters. `field_distributions.json` cited for percentiles but absent from `docs/analysis/benchmarks/`.
