# S-02 — Soft room / reach / trap scores

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L24, L05, L20 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Price cramped or enclosed moves with a bounded score term (room shortfall, reach change, trap weight, trap slack,
kelp-edge surcharge) instead of filtering them out.

## What it computes / does
- ares-v11: room probe over the full post-move body; farm discount withheld when room < body. v12: withheld whenever
  room < required room. v15: bounded score ∝ post-move reach change (weight 0.35) while enclosed or entering. v16: V15
  only at reach ≤8. v17: gate ≤15. v18: ≤8 with weight 0.35→1.2.
- renoir-13b: trap check slack len+3→len+1 (13a slack 6 queued); renoir-14b: trap penalty 30→20 (14a 45 queued);
  ra-01: trap_weight 30→60.
- r3-04: inside the cramped-landing trap penalty, landing with <3 known-open edges pays +2.0; r3-05 = r3-03 + r3-04.
- cx-f04: cramped step pays f4_penalty 6 per kelp edge around the landing.
- Hosts: Ares C++ V06/V09 descendants (production line) except cx-f04 (cx chassis, testbed since D-029).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on Ares C++ (production host) at 20-game (teammates) and 80–160-game seeded (lanes) scale; cx-f04
  reject on the chassis.
- Why it stopped: V19 README — "Soft reach-score versions either failed to change this critical hazard or reduced
  deaths at a substantial r100 retention and W/L cost"; V18 — "stronger narrow-gate score worsened both". R-3 on r3-04:
  "hygiene that the economy does not hold"; r3-05: "the two costs compound" (protocol: stop when a stack step fails).
  Renoir/Monoco: cautious moves pay economy "one for one with the deaths it saves" (BASELINES); monotone in trap weight
  (renoir-14b 20 → +0.017, ra-01 60 → −0.084, d3 cross-reference). cx-f04: Slithery wall is "a navigation leak ... not
  a scoring leak".
- Revival condition: L24 — hazard concentrated at reach ≤8 (a real measurement from V17); L05 (0.8) says the fixes
  tried "all pay economy or move nothing"; a revival would need to change *which* pockets are entered (ra-lane). The
  Ares measurements were on the production host; cx-f04 alone was on the wrong host.
