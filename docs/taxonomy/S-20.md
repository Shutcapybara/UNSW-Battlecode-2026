## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: dropped by the out-of-sample rule (29 Sep 00:45) on every host; on Ares also reject on hygiene guards.
- Why it stopped: OOS rule — "tournament maps are unseen"; "no decision may depend on map identity; atlas only with
  hard off-switch". Yeji — "The owner notes the tournament plays out-of-sample maps and asks for broad heuristics only
  ... From s06 the prior is off". Ares V08 — "added map knowledge appears to drive more pearl capture while creating
  dangerous portal/occupancy routes"; "No seed-2 confirmation, combined variant, or tuning pass was run". Router —
  "Ship with the atlas off".
- Revival condition: L08 (0.1 by rule) — "revives only if organisers announce public tournament maps". L26 keeps the
  hygiene result as evidence "that bed pull without safety pricing is the mechanism L11 must avoid". The measurement
  host is not the reason it stopped (it was measured on Ares).
