# S-25 — EW food-density memory (sciel-03a)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject (guard) · **Ledger:** L12 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

A per-dragon, exponentially decayed grid of where pearls have been eaten, used to scale the value of unseen cells and
beds: `food_ew[c] += 1` per pearl eaten on c, × 0.98 per round (half-life ~34 rounds).

## What it computes / does
- Values scaled by clamp(1 + ew_weight × (kernel density − ew_ref), ew_floor, ew_cap) over Chebyshev radius ew_radius.
- Params (single setting, not swept): ew_decay 0.98, ew_credit 1.0, ew_radius 2, ew_weight 0.6, ew_ref 0.5, ew_floor 0.4,
  ew_cap 2.5. CPU 8.68 M max.
- Python precedents: yeji `online_beds` (never measured), chaewon s03 due-bed memory (identical curves, "no effect").

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: REJECT on the guard (h2h_ally > +10 %), economy cleared — "the only mechanism in the programme to clear the
  +0.05 bar" (BASELINES). Measured on Ares C++, the production host; seed 1 only.
- Why it stopped: convergence crowding "as pre-declared" — dragons converge on the same dense cells. Four
  deconfliction layers ([S-22](S-22.md)) then failed to hold the guard: "valuation-time deconfliction cannot hold the
  guardrail. The collisions are arrival-time".
- Revival condition: L12 (0.7) — "falsified if economy flat with the term on" (it was not flat). The standing next step
  is arrival-level target claims (sciel-05, [S-23](S-23.md)) or L33 warding, then D-032 seeds 1–3. BASELINES calls it
  "the strongest evidence that gains are in state/features, not rules".
