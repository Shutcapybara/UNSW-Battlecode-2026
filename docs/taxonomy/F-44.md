# F-44 — Portal-step walk (C1-D)

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L06 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Exact per-step portal transit reconstruction from replays, with deaths within two rounds of the dragon's own portal step and same-pair friendly double use.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: analysis; `tools/replay_stats/portal_deaths.py` → `build/c1d/portal_deaths.json`; reused by R-3 (`game_stats/runs/r3-v06-portal.json`) and cx-f arena (`portal_steps`, `_walk_portals()`).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-d01-portals.md` §1; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md` §Step 1; `docs/findings/2026-09-29-r4-01-tooling.md` (restored counter).

## What it computes
- Computation: every move simulated through the replay's edge map (4.74 M walks, 0 mismatches; R-3 2.38 M, 0 mismatches); per side-game steps/game, near-deaths (≤ 2 rounds after own portal step) by cause (wall, self, ally-body, h2h), per-100-steps rate, same-pair doubles within 3 rounds; per map and by death round.
- CPU: offline.
- Consumers: [A-27](A-27.md) (portal rows in lane gates; target "−25 % portal-step deaths pooled, ≤ 10 % pearls r100 loss"), [F-43](F-43.md). No bot consumer.
- Evidence it matters (as an instrument): it separated volume from per-transit survival — team 7 ranked 28.2 deaths/100 steps at 49 steps/game vs team 306 12.5 at 47; Ares V06 29.4 at 62.5; chassis 25.9 at 6 steps/game. Every pre-entry fix then measured as volume reduction with flat or worse per-100 rate ([F-18](F-18.md)), which is the basis of D-035's closure.
- Flags: vendored `analyse()` counts steps 5–15 % lower (283 vs 336 for game 497728 B). A `-X ours` merge dropped the cx/f counter on main (dead `portal_steps`), restored in R-4.
