# F-19 — Sonar echo counts

**Kind:** Feature · **Status:** in use (stored everywhere; read by a few lineages) · **Ledger:** L06 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

The five counts (kelp, ally, ally_head, enemy, enemy_head) of what the sender's rays stopped on last turn, aggregated over all rays and carrying no direction.

## What it computes
- Computation: engine-provided 5-tuple; attribution requires casting one ray (cx-f02, kraken, heimdall v10 isolated scan every 4th turn, chaewon-y04). Heimdall: activity = enemy + 2·enemy_head → bed_wait = max(0, 12 − 6·activity); threat × (1 + min(0.18, 0.025·enemy + 0.06·enemy_head)); v10 lanes: directional lead TTL 2, lane cost 1.6 head / 0.35 body × 0.55^age. Kraken: enemy on the ray ⇒ every cell of the ray (W+H) becomes ray_hot → hunter target w_ray 55.
- CPU: "the sonar ray costs ~0.05 M" (cx-f02); Q7: per-ray cost −0.6…+1.2 ± 0.5 M (zero within error).
- Consumers: [A-10](A-10.md) (cx-f02 / chaewon probe, r3-02 exit gate), [A-03](A-03.md) (heimdall bed horizon), [A-11](A-11.md) (heimdall threat multiplier; hydra-v06 stalker-flee), [A-04](A-04.md) (heimdall lane cost), [A-02](A-02.md) (kraken ray_hot target), [A-12](A-12.md) (probe scheduling), [A-23](A-23.md) (m01 features). No consumer in Ares V06.
- Evidence it matters: heimdall v10 12–6 vs Bifröst, 11–7 vs Fenrir v18; champion panel 91–59 (60.7 %); "performance moved materially with bed timing". chaewon-y04 probe +0.062 (20/14, p 0.39); sakura-s02 portal-safety arm (atlas+probe+HOLD) net −9.0 with +3.5 portal-step deaths; cx-f02 probe: "the suppressor is the probe requirement itself (… any dragon on the far line — usually an ally on swarm maps — dirties the echo)"; r3-02 econ −0.120. kraken radar never measured as a component.
- Flags — unused state: `echoes` in every Ares build. With 1–4 broadcast rays per turn (Ares, host) echoes are not attributable to a direction. heimdall echo constants hard-coded outside params and never swept. kraken saved sonar origin is the pre-move head → ray reconstruction off by one step (LINEAGE_REVIEW K4).
