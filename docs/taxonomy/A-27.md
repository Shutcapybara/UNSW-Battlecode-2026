# A-27 — Evaluation protocol (gates, panels, OOS rule, live pipeline)

**Kind:** Algorithm · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L19, L21 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

How a candidate is accepted: the panels it plays, the metrics and gate lines it must clear (+0.05 economy → D-032 interval gate → D-036 local hold), the out-of-sample rule, and the live register/screen/confirmation pipeline.

## What it computes / does
- +0.05 gate (BENCHMARKS step 4): accept when tier-1 econ~ (mean of pearls@50/100/150/250 each ÷ field per-map median) rises ≥ +0.05, dragons and length r100 not falling, no tier-2 rate up > 10 %, panel win not down; else rerun seed 2. Panels: z1 pool = 8 bots × 10 live maps × 2 seats, seed 1 (160 games); gen = 4 opponents × 31 off-pool maps × 2 seats (248; R-4 uses 29 maps).
- D-032 interval gate (29 Sep 23:40): paired fixtures, seeds 1–3, pool + gen, both seats; bootstrap 90 % LB of Δecon > 0 on pool; gen Δecon LB > −0.02; units@100 and length@100 LB ≥ −0.02; no tier-2 rate up > 10 %; win LB > −0.02; per-checkpoint and phase-aware judging. Promotion to the dev screen: stack vs V06 at +0.05 on both panels. ≈1,200 games ≈ 25 min at ~2,900 games/h.
- D-036 local hold: gain transfers within a structural-signature cluster, pooled interval not negative → tested next as a structure-gated switch (signature: tile count, kelp density, corridor degree, portals, bed density, contact distance; esquie 21 features).
- OOS rule (29 Sep 00:45): no decision depends on map identity; atlas only behind a hard off-switch; targets are functions of observable structure; gen panel in every measurement.
- R-4 scorecard GATE: fail = econ down or any tier-2 up > 10 % (incl. from zero) or panel win down; pass = econ ≥ +0.05 with dragons/length not falling; hold otherwise.
- Live pipeline v2/v2.1: metered preflight (Schooltime A, Portals B, + Slithery A, Trauma B) vs sinbad-v07, max < 80 M, p99 < 60 M; screen 3 opponents × 10 pairs (futility ≤ −4); confirmation 12 × 10 (mean ≥ +0.03, p ≤ 0.025, no map ≤ −0.25); v2.1 6 blocks × 20 pairs, ≥ 4/6 positive, interims at 3 and 5.
- Varied: gate line +0.05 → D-029.2 retention exemption → D-032 interval → D-036 local hold; seat-A 80-game early stop (ra lane); panels (pool vs gen; pub/*_rec excluded after the atlas matched them); pipelines (ra/R-4 median-of-side-games vs lune mean).
- Never varied / not adopted: seed-1 default for screens; the +0.05 bar value itself; map weighting by slope² (s1-Q2 proposal); phase-aware gate implementation (R-1 proposal); median vs mean convention (Sciel note); R-4b interval GATE line and corpse-share diagnostic (not delivered); D-032 re-scores of renoir 17a/17d/18a/18c, lune late cap, ra-03/ra-05 (not found).
- CPU: gate for runtime p99 < 60 M, max < 80 M (HANDOFF §7).
- Dimension-keyed: references are per public map; gen maps have no field reference (normalised by renoir-00's own medians, `gen_reference.json`).
