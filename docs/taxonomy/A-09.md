# A-09 — Newborn separation

**Kind:** Algorithm · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L30 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Moving a newborn out of its parent's pocket: the parent's escape decision and type-7 handoff, the child's waypoint plan and move bonus, and the lighter yuna newborn-exit rule.

## What it computes / does
- Parent sends type-7 handoff when (crown splitting a larger child) or (non-crown, non-feeder and child spawn constrained: degree ≤ 2 and (3-step area ≤ `escape_spawn_area` 11 or branches ≤ `escape_spawn_branches` 1)); ray fired backwards, retried ≤ `escape_handoff_retries` 2. Child accepts at age 0/1 within tdist ≤ max(1, age) of the tail cell, else infers escape itself.
- Plan: BFS from origin (`escape_origin_depth` 18 / `_nodes` 180) and head (`escape_target_depth` 12 / `_nodes` 120); waypoint among cells ≥ `escape_min_distance` 5 maximising `escape_open_weight` 1.6·min(area,16) + `escape_branch_weight` 2.5·min(br,4) + `escape_exit_weight` 1.0·(maxdeg−2) + `escape_distance_weight` 0.7·min(away,8) + `escape_resource_weight` 0.18·resource − `escape_route_cost` 0.85·route, hysteresis 2.5; ends after `escape_max_turns` 18 or when ≥5 away and open (`escape_open_area` 8). Bonus: `escape_route_weight` 3.5·`escape_decay` 0.91^age ± `escape_push` 2.2·Δorigin − `escape_origin_cost` 2.0 − `escape_parent_cost` 1.2·(4−d) within `escape_parent_radius` 4 − `escape_revisit_weight` 1.4·min(4, visits); fresh pearl ≤ 2 away (value ≥ 8) may pause (budget 6); `escape_portal_bonus` 1.5.
- yuna nb_mode 1: newborn within Chebyshev 3 of parent ignores targets within nb_radius 3 of parent for nb_age 6 turns, nb_push 1.0; nb_mode 2 only if flood room < nb_tight 12.
- Varied: only `escape_spawn_area` among the 28 C++ params (ra-09 15, switch shipped off). Python: the-goat-v04 escape nodes 96/144 (CPU); ouroboros-s02 80/96 nodes, 16 waypoints; yeji-s05 young caps 90/120 for 2 turns; fenrir v3–v7 (stable waypoint, anti-loop, pearl-detour 3→6, strict corridor trigger); yuna x33 nb_tight 20, x35 no push, x36 radius 2, x34 no-nb; odin v05 all-map, v06 NC ≤ 625; sciel-01a siting; V33 type-9 portal commit (`newborn_portal_commit_bonus` 20, route turns 12, handoff age 1).
- Never varied: the other 27 `escape_*` params (all weights, depths, node caps, decay 0.91, max turns 18, parent radius/cost, pause budget, portal bonus), the retry count, the acceptance tolerance.
- Hard-coded: `_topology` probe caps (3,64)/(2,40)/(2,48); unseen share 0.35 in `escape_resource_value`; parent-cost fallback 0.6.
- CPU: two bounded BFS per escaping newborn per turn (≤300 nodes); Python caps cut for the 100 M wall.
- Dimension-keyed: odin v06 NC ≤ 625; type-9 radio gated by 2·NC ≤ 8192.
