# S-07 — Chassis enclosure probe and corpse gate (cx-f01)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L05, L24 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

A per-turn enclosure probe on the cx C++ chassis: when reach falls below min(room_need, len+2) with no tail-chase
cycle, steer to the deepest reachable cell (room weighted ×16); boxed-in split only if the child has ≥1 legal step;
a CRAMP tier keeps dragons out of 1-wide dead ends.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: C1-F lane cx-f01-escape and cx-f01b-roomneed on cx-f00-base (= anna-a02 chassis); folded into cx-f05
  combined. Host: cx chassis — demoted to testbed by D-029 (not the production line).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-f01-leaks.md` §F-1; `bots/cx-f01-escape/`; C1-C fix list item 1
  (`docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md`).

## What it computes / does
- Time-aware reach flood each turn; enclosed = reach < min(room_need, len+2) and no tail-chase cycle.
- Steer: deepest reachable cell, room ×16. Boxed-in split admitted only if the child has a legal step.
- f01b: room-need variant — bit-identical to f01 on smoke fixtures.
- Pre-registered acceptance (C1-C): trapped len/1k −30 % on Portals/Slithery AND pearls r100 not down.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject (neutral) on the cx chassis, 240 paired fixtures.
- Why it stopped: "the ledger's trapped numbers measured yuna/live, and the chassis already contains the room-check
  tiers that prevent the mill" (cx-f01). Then D-029 made Ares the production line and "cx parts transfer as
  `params.hpp` switches"; leaks re-targeted at Ares as R-3 (D-029 addendum).
- Side finding kept as a rule: "never gate the trapped split by room size — it is Slithery's survival pump (356
  trapped vs 27 greedy splits per 100 rounds; gating by split_room_min took u25 from 25 to 3 and eliminated the team
  at r28)". Bug found: `split_has_room` at len 2 reversed iterator ranges → libc++ `length_error` swallowed by the
  runner catch-all ([P-19](P-19.md)).
- Revival condition: measured on the wrong host for the leak — the chassis did not carry the leak (C1-F: "the ledger's
  leaks are in the yuna-lineage code, not the chassis"); R-3 confirmed Ares V06 carries it (trapped 36.1 vs top-10
  18.5). The probe itself was never ported to Ares as such; the Ares-side equivalents are [S-01](S-01.md)–[S-03](S-03.md).
  L05 (0.8) keeps the "fixable on Ares" claim open.
