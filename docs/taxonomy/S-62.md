# S-62 — Sprint discipline (the-goat-v02/v03, cx-f03, ra-03)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** hold · **Ledger:** L20 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Make multi-step sprints (moves of 2–3 cells that cost a segment each) rarer or only where they win a contested race, to
cut the "sprint tax" — length spent per pearl eaten.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: the-goat-v02/v03/v04 on fenrir-v20 (Python) and the-goat C++ room-rescue v01; cx-f03/f03b on the cx chassis
  (C++ testbed); monoco ra-03 on Ares V06 (C++, production line). Related: sakura-s02 funded sprints (reject, [S-27](S-27.md)).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-29-the-goat-v02-sprint-discipline.md`; `docs/the-goat-family.md`;
  `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-f01-leaks.md` §F-3; `origin/r/monoco:claude/monoco-status.md` (ra-03);
  `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md` (sprint tax); Ares `policy.hpp:1315-1320` (c1 notes).

## What it computes / does
- the-goat: w_sprint 1.0 → 1.5 (v02), 2.0 (v03); v04 adds bounded CPU caps.
- cx-f03: exact engine simulation of the move path; sprint to a pearl/bed only when it wins a contested arrival race
  (f03: any enemy within equal terrain distance, k ≤ 3, cap 4/24 rounds; f03b: gap ≤ 1, k = 2, cap 2/24), no danger
  cells, room check at landing.
- ra-03: Ares `sprint_cost` 1.0 → 1.5 (switch `monoco_sprint_discipline`). Ares sprints exist only as evasion/strike
  candidates near threats (no sprint toward food outside threat — c1 notes).
- Measure: sprint/pearl — team 7 live 0.023 vs band median 0 (q3 0.013); by submission 9508 0.005, 10473 0.029, 10357
  0.044 (C1-C).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: hold. the-goat-v02 retained natively but not runtime-ready; cx-f03 rejected on the chassis; ra-03 rejected
  under the +0.05 bar on the production host but is the ledger's named re-score candidate under D-032.
- Why it stopped: the-goat — "move runtime work to a lighter host or compiled chassis; do not promote or combine
  mechanisms until a candidate clears the pinned gate" (runtime-gate failure on the Fenrir host); cx-f03 — "sprinting in
  any disciplined form (a contested pearl pays 1 segment for a denial that does not materialise)"; the chassis
  "never sprints = correct until arrival-margin model"; ra-03 — "the economy gain missed +0.05; no generalisation run".
- **Host flags:** the-goat stopped by the Python CPU limit — the reason C++ removes (its C++ attempt, room-rescue v01, was
  a different policy). cx-f03 was measured on the testbed chassis whose own sprint volume is near zero. ra-03 is the
  only production-host test and was judged under the superseded gate: L20 — "revives only through the D-032 interval gate
  (ra-03, ra-05 to be re-scored)"; "ra-03 (sprint cost 1.0→1.5, +0.021 both seeds) is the first Monoco candidate to
  re-score under the interval gate" (ledger log, 29 Sep 23:30). No re-score found (d1 notes).
- Revival condition: the D-032 re-score of ra-03 (seeds 1–3, gen panel); C1-C fix #3 target "halve sprint/pearl".
