# A-22 — Look-ahead and adversarial search

**Kind:** Algorithm · **Status:** closed · **Ledger:** — · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Searching beyond one move: per-first-action beam forecasts, reply (alpha-beta) search against a nearby enemy, self-trap continuation look-ahead, planned portal trips, and boost-trap attacks.

## What it computes / does
- Riptide: for every legal first action (incl. sprints ≤ 2) a beam of 8 states over horizon 6 vs stationary bodies; future_discount 0.86; pearl 10, step cost 0.75, frontier 3, risk 10, visit 0.12; fork_food catchment radius 7 / window 25; split stop 350, reserve length 12, pop max 64. x02 adds own-tail-release capacity flood (projected length + escape_margin 4, cap 64).
- Charybdis: own dragon + nearest completely observed enemy within 5 steps (+ simulated children), exact ID turn order; alpha-beta over 3 reply half-turns, width 6, node budget 450 (prototype 1800); value material 3/seg + life 7/producer + mobility 1.2 + target 0.75 + late quadratic bank 0.03 − outside risk 8; growers 1 in 7 ids stop splitting after r90 at len ≥ 6.
- Estuary continuation: node-bounded (120) depth-5 existence search on bodies ≥ 8, one-time pearl consumption, unknown = not trapped, trap_cost 35.
- hunter: ≤6-step boost leaving the enemy head's three sides occupied or ≤1 exit, size lead ≥4 (surround) / ≥3 (cutoff), id-order gated; 6-move self-trap look-ahead.
- Varied: Riptide horizon 1 (myopic) vs 6, early-bank, monolith, factory; x01 → x02 capacity filter; Charybdis reply_plies 0 (control) vs 3, budget 1800 → 450; Estuary safety 0/1.
- Never varied: beam width 8, discount 0.86, Charybdis width 6 and duel range 5, the stationary-body assumption, hunter's 6-ply depth and size-lead thresholds (no ablation in READMEs).
- CPU: Riptide p50 5.7–11.3 M, p99 9.8–15.6 M, max 17.6 M; Charybdis 1800-node 99.8 M, 450 expansion-only 94.9 M, leaf-accounted final p99 5.8–34.4 M, max 57.0 M; Estuary Big Empty max 98.8 M.
- Dimension-keyed: leviathan "compact" reporting (≤625) only.
