# F-46 — Fight anatomy (C2)

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use (diagnostic) · **Ledger:** L09, L10 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Contact and fight events reconstructed from replays, with initiator, outcome, convergence and economic aftermath per fight.

## Where it lives

- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/C2-fight-anatomy.md`; C2-0 status (d3 notes); D-030.

## What it computes
- Computation: contact = wrap-aware Manhattan ≤ 4, window 6, group ≥ 3, 5-round closure, adjacency-based initiator; deaths priced to last contact + 5. Per side-game: fights/game, initiator share, trade / kill-for / kill-against / disengage shares, own deaths and length lost per fight, trade-ahead share, convergence ratio (pre-contact heads toward centre), conv ≥ 2 share, synced entries, rays/head pre-fight, pearl Δ next 10 rounds, bed flip. 182,788 contact events over 10,331 games.
- CPU: offline.
- Consumers: [A-27](A-27.md) / director decisions (D-030 dropped the S-3 leader protocol on its falsifier). The proposed pricing rules (converge ≥ 2 heads or refuse; never trade when behind in units; don't initiate > 6 cells from a bed) were never built — no bot consumer.
- Evidence it matters: top-10 vs band — group fights 8.96 vs 8.58/side-game; convergence 0.29 vs 0.27; initiate at even parity 50.3 vs 50.9 %; arrive ahead in units 53.6 vs 44.8 %; trade while ahead 55.1 vs 44.2 %; net kill +3.5 pp vs −0.4 pp; next-10 pearl swing +1.08 vs −0.81; initiate when bed > 6 cells 38.5 vs 54.8 %. Team 7: initiation 48.3 %, fights 7.55/game, convergence 0.18 (compact 0.12 vs 0.22), 11.6 own deaths and 32.9 length lost per fight vs band 6.8 / 18.9. Verdict S-3: "a pricing rule, not a protocol".
- Flags: portals not followed in distance; ~60 % of pooled participant deaths double-claimed across overlapping events.
