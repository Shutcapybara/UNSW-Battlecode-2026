## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on Ares C++ (production) and the chassis; Python spacing terms accepted on their own hosts (older,
  weaker lineages).
- Why it stopped: router — crowding rules "reduced deaths but cost more pace than they saved. Now off". Monoco — ra-07
  moved ally head-on −2.1 % "vs predicted 10 %", i.e. "failed to move their intended statistic enough". Renoir — single
  knobs "3–5× short of the gate" (L04/L20). Sciel's later diagnosis applies to all scalar crowd terms: "The collisions
  are arrival-time: targets were chosen when the ... convergers are beyond the 7×7 view" ([S-22](S-22.md)). Yuna x08:
  "harmful in combo".
- Revival condition: L20 (0.1) for single knobs; L33 (0.4) proposes signed coupling over sonar (warding) as the
  structural successor — "start with two entries: explore→explore and transit→transit negative within k cells".
