# P-13 — Crown / conversion at r500

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L15, L18 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Games that reach the round limit are decided by the longest single dragon; teams that lead on total material lose the tiebreak because length is not concentrated (or the concentrated dragon dies) by r500.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: hunter-v14/v20, avery-v03/v05, valjean-v01, gavroche-v17, bifrost-v01 (live 8540), Heartbreaker (field), yuna (maze maps), spike, tidus; crown mechanisms in ouroboros v02/v09/v10, avery v04–v08, Tyr/Ares crown beacons.
- Code / docs: `docs/public-replay-review-2026-09-25.md`; `experiment_data/team_recon_62_*` main; `experiment_data/team_recon_7_20260928_claude` §4, §9; `experiment_data/temporal_policy_20260927T173800Z_yuna/REPORT.md`; `docs/ouroboros-design.md` §6.

## How it is measured
- Round-limit losses with a total-material lead, and the longest-dragon gap at r500 (recon and lineage reviews); crown survival and paired crown margin r400/450/500 (jet `crown.py`) — no single instrument; outcome side in [F-50](F-50.md).
- Early concentration `top1_share@100` (down is better) — [F-42](F-42.md) tier 1.
- Three-number form: `top1_share@100` zoo 0.11 · top-10 0.08 · field 0.10; percentile **not given**. Other crown metrics: not given in three-number form.
