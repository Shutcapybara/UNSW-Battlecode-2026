# S-51 — Field-relative benchmarks replacing zoo ratings (BENCHMARKS; zoo retired)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L19, L21 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Stop ranking bots by win share against the team's own zoo and score candidates instead on metrics normalised by the
field's per-map medians (economy curve, hygiene rates, outcome proxies), chosen for carrying across maps and matching
live signs.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: F1 feature lab (Claude) → BENCHMARKS; R-4 scorecard; every Ares lane since 29 Sep. Retired: local ratings
  (`performance_model` / `benchmark_ratings`), adaptive tournament scheduler, 24-opponent curation pool.
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md`; `docs/analysis/benchmarks/field_references.json`,
  `map_reference_medians.json`; `docs/TEAM-SUMMARY-2026-09-29.md` §1.1; D-010, D-017;
  `docs/findings/2026-09-28-analysis-claude-Q4-live-local-transfer.md`, `…-Q10-rating-table.md`;
  `docs/archive/adaptive-tournament-20260929.md`; `docs/frontier-panel-20260929.md`.

## What it computes / does
- Tier 1 economy: pearls at r50/100/150/250 and dragons/length at r100, each ÷ the field's per-map median ("map share of
  variance falls from 0.74 to 0.13"); tier 2 hygiene rates (kelp, self, ally-body, head-on, newborn); tier 3 outcome
  proxies (diagnostic only). Metrics selected on six measured properties including "Carries across maps" and "Same sign
  live".
- The opponent set of the fixed panel is still zoo bots (z1: 7–8 opponents × 10 maps × 2 seats); only the yardstick is
  the field.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: in use — "We stopped using the zoo as a fitness function on 28 Sep" (TEAM-SUMMARY); BENCHMARKS is the
  yardstick for every lane; the adaptive tournament "Stopped at user request" (28 Sep 19:13 UTC; archive).
- Why the predecessor stopped: "Everything evolved against our own bot zoo … optimised for beating ourselves"
  (TEAM-SUMMARY §1.1); ATLAS Q10: "Must not order the queue; panel bias, not sparsity, is the defect".
- Open items: `field_distributions.json`, cited by BENCHMARKS for percentiles, is not present under
  `docs/analysis/benchmarks/` (d4 notes); tier-2 ICC 0.91–0.98 but "not rating-predictive"; the fixed panel's opponents
  are still zoo bots, so the gap between local panels and the live band persists (clone-62 was built as a band proxy,
  "every future candidate should be screened against it" — not adopted into z1, d2 notes).
- Revival condition: L19 (0.6) — the dev-545 live screen is the transfer check, untestable while the executor is in
  shadow ([S-52](S-52.md)).

## Discrepancies
- "Deficit is deaths, not income" (TEAM-SUMMARY §1.3) vs Sophie K-1 "losing maps are lost to opening economy and
  production" (ρ +0.90 economy vs −0.66 avoidable deaths) — both read off field-relative metrics (d1 Contradictions).
- R-1: arena-based and replay-extractor normalised levels differ by ~20 % on identical games.
