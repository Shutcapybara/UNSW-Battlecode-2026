# P-03 — Portal-exit deaths (per-transit hazard, same-pair doubles)

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L06, L23, L05 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Dragons die within two rounds of their own portal step — into kelp, their own body, an ally body, or a head-on at a blind landing — at about twice the per-transit rate of the strongest team; two allies diving the same pair and meeting at the exit ("same-pair double") is a named sub-case.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: team-7 live (all 28 Sep submissions), yuna/tyr/chaewon hosts, Ares V06 and lanes, ouroboros-v10/s02, sinbad/MC/valjean (Python "box paralysis" vs reckless dives); anna chassis at 6 steps/game shows little of it.
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-d01-portals.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md` §Step 1–2; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-f01-leaks.md` §F-2; `docs/findings/2026-09-29-ouroboros-s02-portal.md`.

## How it is measured
- Defining measurement (rate form): deaths within 2 rounds of the dragon's own portal step per 100 portal steps, with transit volume (steps/game) reported beside it and same-pair double-use deaths within 3 rounds — [F-44](F-44.md) portal-step walk (`tools/replay_stats/portal_deaths.py`; 4.74M walks, 0 mismatches).
- Ledger form: length lost per 1k dt r0–99 by dragons dying with head ≤2 cells of a portal (geographic) — [F-43](F-43.md) "portal" class.
- Three-number form: absolute · gap to the best comparator (team 306, rank 1; top-10 for the ledger row) · band value. Field percentile **not given**.

## Solutions tried
- [S-15](S-15.md) pre-entry pair memory / exit-known (cx-f02, r3-01, r3-02) — reject: r3-02 portal row −56% by cutting transits −68%, per-100 29.4→32.6, econ −0.120 ("the exit is not made safe; it is made unfamiliar").
- [S-16](S-16.md) blind-exit occupancy memory (valjean, witten-x01, scholze-v03, einstein-v02, skadi, bifrost) — mixed small-panel: valjean dilemma+autarky 3–21 → 14–10; einstein null; witten-x01 "do not promote".
- [S-17](S-17.md) one-ray probe and HOLD packet (chaewon y04/y05, ares-v07-hold-only, ouroboros-s02) — reject on Ares: ally head-on −13.2%, econ +0.005, expected score −5 pts.
- [S-18](S-18.md) post-transit navigation (sciel-02a) — reject: portal row −21%, econ −0.004, units@100 −0.057.
- [S-19](S-19.md) dive caps / phase-scheduled portal policy (sinbad v_dive 3, yuna x01/v05) — accept in Python lines: sinbad portal maps 36–20 → 45–11; yuna x01 +4/80.
- [S-14](S-14.md) portal route handoff at split (ares-v33) — local only.
