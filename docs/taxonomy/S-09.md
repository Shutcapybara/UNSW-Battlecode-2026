# S-09 — Pocket-farm discount changes

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L29, L24 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Change the host's farm discount — the trap penalty is multiplied by 0.15 when a pocket's pearls reach split length and
the tail can escape — by removing it, conditioning it on room or visible pearls, or adding dead-end farming.

## Where it lives
- Lineages:
  - Ares C++ (production line): Renoir `ra` renoir-03a (farm 0.5, unreported), 03b farm10 (trap_farm_factor 0.15→1 =
    farming off), 24 farm2 (discount only if pocket ≥2 visible pearls); ares-v12 (no farm credit when room < need).
  - Python: ouroboros-v07-forage2 (dead-end farming + emergency split), v08-contest (farm_max_len 5); pace-v03 (no farm
    discount to r250); bifrost-v04/v29 net-growth farms; skadi-v07 productive farm; chaewon-s02 farm_min_region 3;
    valjean dead-end pearl devaluing.
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-ra-lane.md` (rows 03b, 24, §Churn); `bots/ares-v12-no-underroom-farm-credit/README.md`;
  `docs/ouroboros-design.md`; `docs/findings/2026-09-29-pace-v01.md`; `docs/bifrost-family.md`; `docs/serre.md` §3.

## What it computes / does
Host rule (Tyr/Ares, sinbad origin v03 e7): trap × 0.15 for eat-and-split-out pockets. Variants either turn it off
(03b, pace-v03), gate it (24 on visible pearls; ares-v12 on room; bifrost on net +1 segment after the escape split;
skadi-v07 on productivity; chaewon on region size), or add farming behaviour (ouroboros v07).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on both hosts; Ares measurements are on the production line (seeded 80–160-game panels).
- Why it stopped: Renoir — "Farming alone is worth 0.62 econ~ on Autarky"; "On Ares the C1-C leaks are the economy's own
  exhaust. A leak fix has to change which pockets are entered, not how many" (ra-lane §Churn). Serre: "devaluing
  pocket/dead-end pearls (halves intake — pocket farming *is* the economy)". Pace: farm discount is not what drives
  the Slithery mill.
- Revival condition: L29 (0.8, established) — pearls@k rewards churn, so a farm change must hold units/length guards;
  a pocket-*selection* rule (which pockets) remains untried (ra-lane recommendation, inferred as the open form).
