# F-42 — Tier-2 hygiene rates and newborn10

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L29 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Self-inflicted death rates per 1k dragon-turns (wall/kelp, own body, ally body, ally head-on, invalid) and newborn deaths within 10 rounds per 100 births, used as guardrails beside the economy tier.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: analysis (BENCHMARKS tier 2); consumed by ra/renoir lane, monoco, sciel (`origin/r/sciel:tools/sciel/lane.py`), R-4 scorecard, esquie, sakura-s02 `panel.py`, kazuha/sakura/yeji panels (yeji `deaths.py` distinguishes forced vs chosen deaths).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` §"The benchmark set", table "Death rate", guardrails; `docs/analysis/FEATURES.md` survival table; Q1 §5, Q5 (d2 notes).

## What it computes
- Computation: tier 1 economy (econ curve, units@100|map, total@100|map, births@100|map, top1_share@100 down); tier 2 hygiene (death_wall_per1k, death_self_per1k, death_ally_body_per1k, death_h2h_ally_per1k, death_invalid_per1k; `avoidable_deaths_per1k` = their sum); `newborn_deaths10_per100`; tier 3 proxies for a fixed panel only (bed_capture_share, pearls@150|rel, territory@100, total_share@250). Hygiene rates are side-game **means**; economy uses medians (mixed statistics).
- CPU: offline.
- Consumers: [A-27](A-27.md) (guard: no tier-2 rate up > 10 %, including from zero; D-032 lower bounds). No bot consumer.
- Evidence it matters: tier-2 rates are the most stable metrics (seed ICC 0.91–0.98, opponent share ≤ 0.09) but team correlation with rating ≈ 0 — "hygiene, not strategy"; within-game +0.20 log-odds live. Levels: zoo avoidable 12.97 / winners 12.66 / top-10 9.83 per 1k; zoo kelp 4.0 and self 3.0 vs top-10 0 and 0. newborn10: zoo 33, winners 31, pooled field ~34, Slithery/Portals 48–49. As a guard it decided outcomes: sciel-03a (h2h_ally +34 %), renoir-07a (+51 %), esquie-04 (own-body +24 %), r3-03 (own-body +11 %, hold).
- Flags: "Fewer self-inflicted deaths by playing small" — kazuha-s01 7.9/1k wins 19 %, hunter-v20 9.7/1k wins 12 % (guardrail caveat). Friendly-kill rates are +0.5 to +1.2/SD in replay associations ("busy, winning swarms also bump into themselves more"). ouroboros-m01 invalid 10.9/1k called "a bug" but is deliberate `SPLIT 1`.
