# P-24 — Body suppression of bed spawns

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** never measured · **Ledger:** — · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

The engine rejects a pearl spawn when any dragon segment occupies the bed and then resets the countdown, so dragons that sit on or pass through due beds suppress supply; observed in public replays, never measured as a bot mechanism.

## Where it lives
- Engine: `TrySpawnPearl` (per the public replay review).
- Code / docs: `docs/public-replay-review-2026-09-25.md` §3; `docs/analysis/FEATURES.md` Known limitations ("Realised spawns are suppressed while a bed is occupied").

## How it is measured
- Count of scheduled bed renewals blocked by bodies per game, by side, from replay spawn events (public replay review) — no F-4x instrument computes it; [F-45](F-45.md) `density_ratio` uses nominal bed rates and inherits the limitation.
- Three-number form: not given.

## Evidence
| Measurement | Value | Base / panel | Source |

## Solutions tried
- None. PRR states a candidate hypothesis: "approach due beds without occupying them"; the related bed-timing work ([S-27](S-27.md)) values arrival but does not model occupancy suppression.

## Status and open questions
- Never measured as a mechanism. Open questions: how often our own bodies block our own beds on bed-dense maps (Schooltime, Slithery, Devil); whether crowding on beds ([P-04](P-04.md)) reduces supply as well as causing collisions.
