# P-02 — Newborn deaths

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L05, L30 (no own row) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Split children die within 10 rounds of birth; the length they carried is lost, often into the parent's own pocket or a crowded exit.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: every splitting line; measured on team-7 live, yuna-v03/v05, Ares V01–V06 and lane bases, S1 generation on ouroboros-v10, ouroboros/hydra/leviathan/kraken (LINEAGE_REVIEW), fafnir/newton (arena).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md` §priority list; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md` §Step 1; `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` guardrails; `docs/analysis/C1-pace-targets.md` Reading 12; S1 findings `docs/findings/2026-09-28-{kazuha,sakura,chaewon,yeji}-s01*.md`.

## How it is measured
- Defining measurement (leak row): length lost per 1k dragon-turns r0–99 by children that died ≤10 rounds after birth — [F-43](F-43.md) ledger class "newborn".
- Rate form: `newborn_deaths10_per100` = children dead within 10 rounds per 100 births — [F-42](F-42.md) (BENCHMARKS guardrail against "splitting into dust").
- Three-number form: absolute · gap to top-ten · band value. Field percentile **not given** for either form.

## Status and open questions
- Open. BENCHMARKS uses the rate as a guardrail only; the P1 pace prompt's ceiling (≤10/100 births) is "far stricter than the field's own practice" (38.5/100).
- Untested: child's first target in the birth packet (sciel-01a follow-up); siting on `ACT:tsplit` children (L30 pay-down route).

## Discrepancies
- Top-10 lose *more* newborn length than the band pooled (24.0 vs 18.4): "the top ten do not churn less — they eat faster and replace" (cx-c01). The gap is map-concentrated.
- Newborn/100 births on the same V06 base: sciel-00 34.6 vs r3-03 comparison "V06 25.0" — different pipelines (lane.py copy vs r3 ledger).
- Certificate delivery rate: kazuha "reaches every child on its first turn"; chaewon 35–49% of splits; yeji 37%; ouroboros "half never arrive" — "the three numbers cannot all be right" (director S1 evaluation).
