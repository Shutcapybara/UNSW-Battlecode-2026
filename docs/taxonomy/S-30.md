## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on Ares C++ (production host), two independent lanes; three hygiene holds committed OFF.
- Why it stopped: Renoir — "The +0.05 single-seed bar is out of reach for one-mechanism patches on a tuned Tyr
  descendant"; paired bootstrap ±0.03 on 160 games, so a single seed resolves +0.05 only for true effects ~+0.08;
  "seed shifts the world by +0.07" (renoir-00 econ~ 1.111 seed 1 vs 1.182 seed 2); seat-A screens overstate by ~half.
  Monoco — "the accepted stack adds nothing to V06"; "The next useful exploration should prioritize a direct economy
  improvement". R-index — "V06 is near a local optimum for one-knob moves". Renoir lane closed by D-034.
- Revival condition: L20 (0.1 dormant) — "revives only through the D-032 interval gate (ra-03, ra-05 to be re-scored)"
  or "if a knob shows a consistent seat-independent +0.03"; L04 (0.6) routes the same weights to a joint tuner (R-5 →
  SF-1), never run. The D-032 re-scores were not found in the corpus.
