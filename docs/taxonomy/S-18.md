# S-18 — Post-transit navigation (sciel-02a)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L06, L31 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

For a few rounds after the dragon's own portal transit, steer it to clear the exit mouth, penalise staying in the mouth
zone and brake re-transit — an exit-side rule, decayed over time and suppressed during newborn escape.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: Sciel lane (GLM, R-2) sciel-02a-ptnav on sciel-00-base (= Ares V06 atlas-off). Ares C++, production line.
- Code / docs: `origin/r/sciel:docs/findings/2026-09-30-sciel-lane.md`; `origin/r/sciel:claude/sciel-status.md`;
  `origin/r/sciel:bots/sciel-02a-ptnav/params.hpp`. Motivation: R-3 open item 1 ("post-transit navigation (first 2–3
  steps after transit) — untested").

## What it computes / does
- For `pt_rounds` after own transit: per-step distance-from-landing clearing term (`pt_clear_weight`), mouth-zone malus
  (`pt_mouth_radius`, `pt_mouth_cost`), re-transit brake (`pt_reenter`), all × `pt_decay` per round.
- Params (single setting): pt_rounds 4, pt_decay 0.6, pt_clear_weight 1.2, pt_mouth_radius 1, pt_mouth_cost 2.0,
  pt_reenter 3.0. No sweep.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: REJECT ("leak moved, not closed") on Ares C++ (production host), seed 1, pool + gen.
- Why it stopped: "steering off mouths and braking re-transits gives hygiene at an economy cost on the maps where
  transits are productive"; "pooled portal leak is only 6.3 len/1k to harvest" (sciel-status). No parameter sweep
  followed.
- Revival condition: L06 (0.2) cites it: "Sciel-02a post-transit steering moved the row −21 % and paid economy.
  Revival only as an exit-side mechanism that improves per-transit survival at unchanged volume" — this *is* the
  exit-side shape; the untested part is a setting that keeps economy flat (no sweep). L31 (state machines, 0.5) cites
  02a's decayed steering as a two-state machine that moved its row — the mode version is the proposed next form.
