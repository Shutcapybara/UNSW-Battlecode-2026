## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on every chassis measured; porthos P1 is the one accept, and von_neumann later showed its push term
  inert and the aggro-off arm no worse.
- Why it stopped: godel — "x14 fails the frozen gauntlet gate (112 < 115 = baseline + 3). No promotion"; x27 "missed the
  frozen advance bar (31 < 32)"; godel §6 — "aggression is a valid component to keep and calibrate defensively; it is
  not a dial to turn up". von_neumann — "the binding constraint is contact creation (economy-driven), not trade
  admission"; "The swarm push term (P1-1b) is dead in practice"; reserve "cannot express saturation (0 flips both
  cycles)". ouroboros-v06 — "Neither moved the needle; kept as a record".
- Host flag: all Python, on 24–48-game screens with ~σ 4.6 per 90 games; none measured on Ares. The Ares-side
  aggression knobs are [S-38](S-38.md) (threat weight, attack margin), rejected on the production host.
- Revival condition: godel recommends "per-opponent evidence through sonar reports" (team-level style estimate), not
  built; L10 local rules ([S-40](S-40.md)); C2-0: fights cost ~2× the band, a cost problem rather than admission.
