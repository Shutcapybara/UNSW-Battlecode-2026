# F-09 — Room flood (time-aware flood_room, flood_need, caps)

**Kind:** Feature · **Status:** in use (`head_block` branch unreachable) · **Ledger:** L24 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Time-aware flood from a candidate post-move head that counts reachable cells (up to a need threshold) while own and other segments vacate on schedule; the basis of the trap penalty and the split room checks.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: SIN/MC hosts (`tx.flood`), yuna, tyr, fenrir, gavroche, ares-v02..v33 and V06 descendants; anna/cx chassis (room tiers, `split_has_room`, tail-chase flag); ouroboros flood(cell, body, need+1); kraken `flood_space` (len+6); valjean topology.room.
- Code / docs: `bots/ares-v06-expanded-search-support/policy.hpp:828-840` (flood_need), `:922-931` (head block), `:954-957` (credits), `:965` (pocket pearls), `:1393-1403` (trap term); `bots/yuna-v00-control/tactics.py` flood; `bots/sinbad-v07-divecap/policy.py` L343-349; `bots/cx-f00-base/policy.hpp:106-177`.

## What it computes
- Computation: BFS from the post-move head; own segment i blocks while t < i+2, others per `vac` ([F-06](F-06.md)); rim cells with an unknown edge credit `flood_credit` 2, unpaired portal `flood_portal_credit` 6; stops at `need` = max(len + slack 3, min_area 5); len ≥ 10: min(need + len/3, flood_cap_long 40; late ≥150: 24; late+sparse: 32), else min(need, flood_cap 24). Also counts visible pearls inside (pocket, for the farm discount).
- CPU: capped by need; one whole-map 32×32 flood ≈ 0.43 M points (C1-A). Caps are CPU-driven compromises from the Python 100 M wall: Tyr 22/36, tyr-v13 20/32, V06 24/40, V09/robert 32/48, lune ×8 64/112.
- Consumers: [A-04](A-04.md) trap term (trap_weight 30·deficit/need, +30 if cells < len, × farm factor 0.15), [A-06](A-06.md) (child flood ≥ child_area 4; parent deficit), [A-07](A-07.md)/[A-08](A-08.md) (v32 dead-end child search, r3-03 fires at flood < 0.75·need), [A-20](A-20.md) (anna room tiers), [A-19](A-19.md) (router room need).
- Evidence it matters: the thresholds matter in both directions on Ares: renoir-12a min_area 8: −0.183 econ (kelp −20 %); renoir-13b slack 1: +0.008; monoco ra-01 trap_weight 60: −0.0838 (wall −14.36 %) vs renoir-14b 20: +0.017 (monotone in trap weight); renoir-03b farm factor 1.0: −0.058. Flood caps ×8 (lune-05): "every fixture is identical through r100" (inert). r3-03 escape at flood < 0.75·need: win +5.0 pp at two seeds, own-body +11 % (hold, L30).
- Flags: `head_block = 0` in V06 so the "other head may take a neighbour" block (:922-931) never runs (anna chassis had 1; sinbad v02 ablation −8). Rim credit over-counts at the view boundary: scholze LEAK-03 "+8 free-space credits for tiles on the view boundary … 14 of 27 casualties before round 100 had free_exits == 0" (Devil).
