# S-31 — Search depth ladder

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** accept (V06 as base) / reject (ladder) · **Ledger:** L01, L02, L03, L25, L22 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: V06 accepted as the programme base (D-029; R-1 "retention gain off-pool, economy not down"); the uniform
  ladder rejected on the production host (win falls at every level).
- Why it stopped: "The slope is not 'more is better'" — wide search in the first 40 rounds hurts (dragons converge on
  the same distant pearl; newborn cap 60→480 wanders) while late search helps; hence the late-only cap ([S-32](S-32.md)).
  c1 notes: "the next lever is not depth but what and when".
- Revival condition: L02 (0.7) — selective depth keyed on volatility or discrete profiles + a selector ("cap by observed
  local pearl sparsity, not round"); L03 (0.7) phase-conditional logic; L25 (0.5) asks for V09 as a named point on the
  ladder (between L1 and L2) — not reported by R-1.

## Discrepancies
