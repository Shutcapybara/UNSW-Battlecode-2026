# P-12 — Fight cost and weak convergence

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L10, L09, L33 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Our fights cost about twice the band's deaths and length, and fewer of our heads converge on a contact; the top ten's initiator edge is composition (they arrive ahead), not a coordination protocol.

## How it is measured
- C2 fight anatomy — contact events (wrap-aware Manhattan ≤4, window 6, group ≥3, 5-round closure, adjacency initiator; deaths priced to last contact +5): own deaths and length lost per fight, convergence ratio (pre-contact heads toward centre), initiator share, trade-ahead share, next-10 pearl swing — [F-46](F-46.md).
- h2h deaths per 1k dragon-turns in wins vs losses (ATLAS Q1, [F-50](F-50.md)).
- Three-number form: ours · top-10 · band given; field percentile **not given**.
