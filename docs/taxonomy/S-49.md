# S-49 — Gate revisions (+0.05 → D-029.2 → D-032 → D-036)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L21, L20, L29 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Successive changes to the local acceptance rule for a candidate: a single-seed +0.05 economy delta, then an exemption
for retention changes, then a paired multi-seed interval gate with guards and phase-aware judging, then a per-map
"local hold" tier.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: rule set applied to every Ares lane (Renoir, Lune, Sciel, Monoco, R-3, Esquie) and to teammates' screens
  by reference.
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` §"How to use it" step 4; `docs/findings/2026-09-28-director-decisions.md`
  D-029 (2), D-032, D-035 (1), D-036; `docs/hub/HYPOTHESES.md` L21; `docs/BASELINES-2026-09-30.md` "Measure like this";
  `docs/findings/2026-09-29-r4-01-tooling.md` (scorecard `GATE:` line); `docs/findings/2026-09-30-ra-lane.md` §gate proposal.

## What it computes / does
- **+0.05 (BENCHMARKS step 4)**: accept when the tier-1 mean of pearls@50/100/150/250 (÷ field map median) rises by
  ≥ 0.05, dragons and length at r100 not falling, no tier-2 rate up > 10 %, panel win not down; else rerun seed 2.
  Panel 140–160 side-games, seed 1.
- **D-029 (2)**: retention changes judged by "the BENCHMARKS logic, not the +0.05 economy delta" (V06 passes on those
  terms).
- **D-032**: accept into a lane stack on paired fixtures, seeds 1–3, live pool + gen panel, both seats: bootstrap 90 %
  LB of Δecon > 0 (pool), gen Δecon LB > −0.02, units/length@100 LB ≥ −0.02, no tier-2 up > 10 %, win LB > −0.02,
  per-checkpoint deltas, phase-aware; promote to the dev screen at +0.05 for the accumulated stack vs V06.
- **D-036**: third tier "local hold" for structure-cluster gains ([S-46](S-46.md)).

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: in use — D-032 (with D-036) is the lane gate since 29 Sep 23:40 UTC; +0.05 remains the promote-to-dev bar.
- Why it stopped (each prior form): +0.05 — "the seed alone moves the same bot's economy by +0.071"; "fishtest works
  because its bound matches patch size" (D-032); "wrong shape for late-phase and small-patch gains" (L21).
- Open implementation gap: D-035 (1) gave R-4b "the D-032 interval form and a corpse-share diagnostic"; the scorecard's
  interval `GATE:` line and the corpse-share diagnostic were not found delivered, and the re-scores D-032 ordered
  (Renoir 17a/17d/18a/18c, Lune late-cap, Monoco ra-03/ra-05) were not found (d1 notes).
- Revival condition / proposals not adopted: s1-Q2 — weight maps by slope² (a Portals result is worth ~1/3 of a
  Schooltime result in scope); Sciel — "the gate's median-per-side-game convention bites when gains are
  map-concentrated" (04b median +0.039 vs mean +0.103).

## Discrepancies
- D-029.2 had exempted retention changes from +0.05 before D-032; L21's evidence column still reads V06 as "the best
  evidence in the programme and failed it" (d1 notes).
