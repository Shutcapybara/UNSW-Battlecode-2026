## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on every Python host; never measured on Ares (ra-12 interrupted by engine resource errors).
- Why it stopped: b01/b02 — "stopping production earlier costs material. Final total length drops by 13–25 … Timing
  without that scale just converts a smaller army sooner"; yuna — "Donations activate (≈40/game) but do not raise final
  longest"; newton — "Conversion timing is opponent-conditional" (recommended swarm-detector conditioning, not built);
  vicious — "all 33-map confirmation failed"; 470 learned gate keyed on width > 61.5 "cannot reach its 0.2 threshold"
  (a dimension-keyed, measured transfer failure); chaewon — "Stop tuning conversion clocks (rejected on two hosts)".
- Revival condition: b01/b02's named next step is a larger early swarm × earlier conversion 2×2 (Vibing converts
  earlier from a bigger swarm; not in corpus). L15 crown-margin corpus test; the Ares grow_from arm (ra-12) is the one
  untested production-host point.
