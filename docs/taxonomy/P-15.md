# P-15 — Pool fit and the generalisation gap (incl. dev-545 proxy, gen-panel coverage)

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L19, L14, L28 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Gains measured on the fixed local pool (8 zoo opponents × 10 live maps) and on small screens concentrate there and do not carry to unseen maps or to live opponents; the panels meant to catch this do not contain the pool's hardest map motifs, and the live proxy (dev team 545) has one recorded strike.

- Lineages: Ares V06 lanes (renoir, sciel, r3, lune), Python lines (godel, von_neumann, yuna, yeji), leaklab doctrine lines (serre/sinbad/O13).

## How it is measured
- Paired pool Δ vs gen-panel Δ for the same candidate ([F-49](F-49.md)); coverage = share of field side-games outside the zoo's per-map 5–95% range ([F-45](F-45.md) A2 compare); map-signature clusters of pool vs gen maps ([F-48](F-48.md)); dev-screen pass vs field confirmation (L19).
- Three-number form: not applicable; coverage and pool/gen deltas are the measures.
