## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: closed. gavroche-v54 staged, not submitted; the rest are per-lineage CPU fixes.
- Why it stopped: gavroche — "The user instructed that if V66 did not work, stop experimenting and stage the best
  Gavroche version that avoids TLE. V66 failed the priority-family gate, so the campaign is closed"
  (gavroche-resume). Programme level — the problem was removed by the C++ port: "The 100 M cap and the 60 M screening
  wall are not constraints on Ares's search" (r1 finding; unbounded caps 22.4 M). Runtime repairs alone:
  "removing TLE faults does not move outcomes" (Q2 decision, d2 notes).
- **Revival flag — CPU limit removed by C++.** Mechanisms cut here for CPU are candidates for revival on Ares (pa2
  Dead section): the 3-step sprint fan-out (v21 lost 4/4 without it), target search 1,500 → 160 nodes, reverse
  search, flood need caps 24/40, the gaia breeding scan, valjean's never-run topology floods. Partial test on Ares:
  R-1's sprint×8 and flood×8 were identical through r100 ("refutes the earlier hypothesis that the 3-step sprint
  threshold explains the opening loss"); late-cap ×8 helped late ([S-32](S-32.md)). Yuna's bigger caps (−7/−5) and
  R-1's late cap (+) have opposite signs on the two hosts.
- Revival condition: L22 "revives if R-1 shows an early-game loss that is CPU-caused (it is not: 9 M of 100 M)".
