# S-15 — Pre-entry pair memory and exit-known gates

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L06 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Decide *before* entering a portal whether its exit is safe: remember survived transits per pair, charge a cost for an
unverified exit, or skip the entering step unless the landing is known clear; one sonar probe ray per turn through an
adjacent blind portal (chassis).

## Where it lives
- Lineages:
  - cx chassis (testbed): C1-F cx-f02-portals and variants f02b/c/d/e (C1-D §2 rules, implemented by C1-F).
  - Ares C++ (production): R-3 (GLM) r3-01-pair-memory, r3-02-exit-known on V06 atlas-off.
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-f01-leaks.md` §F-2; `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-d01-portals.md` §2–4;
  `docs/findings/2026-09-30-r3-ares-leaks.md` §Step 2; `bots/r3-01-*`, `bots/r3-02-*` params.hpp.

## What it computes / does
- cx-f02: pair exit memory (survived transit = clear 8 rounds; seen-blocked bans the pair 6), probe ray, id parity per
  pair edge, id-order exit simulation, `exit_known(pair)`.
- r3-01: last survived transit per pair; exit danger from enemy DragonMem within 2 of landing; unverified exit
  (>`r3_pair_fresh` 8 rounds) pays `r3_pair_cost` 2.0 + `r3_pair_danger_cost` 4.0. All-off golden = V06 on 15,178 turns.
- r3-02: skip the portal step unless the landing is known clear (terrain seen ≤12 rounds, no body, no remembered danger,
  recent survived transit, or last 4-ray sonar fan enemy-clean); yields only if all else is certain death.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on both hosts; the Ares measurement is at real transit volume (62.5 steps/game) on the production line.
- Why it stopped: cx-f02 — "the only active mechanism in every variant is the memory gate ... a pure volume throttle".
  r3-01 — "the row moved only by transiting 22% less; per-transit survival worsened"; r3-02 — "the clearest sakura
  trap ... The exit is not made safe; it is made unfamiliar". R-3: "Pre-entry portal pricing does not fix Ares's
  portal leak at any setting that moves the row"; per-100-steps invariant 29–33 across every arm. D-035 (3) closed
  crossing-side portal fixes on both hosts.
- Revival condition: L06 (0.2) — "Revival only as an exit-side mechanism that improves per-transit survival at
  unchanged volume". Residual untested items (cx-f02): id-sim at distance 2 with enemy sprint reach; dirty-only-on-enemy
  probe echoes at router volumes; same-pair doubles "need live-swarm volumes". Evidence that the leak is exit-side:
  Sophie K-1 ally head-on at portal mouths ×1.5–1.9.
