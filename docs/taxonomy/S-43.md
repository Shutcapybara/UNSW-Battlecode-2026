# S-43 — Crown election and feeding clocks (bifrost/avery/ouroboros beacon/tidus/hunter-v23)

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L15, L18 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Elect one long dragon (the crown) late in the game, relay it by sonar beacon, stop it splitting and trading, and have
small allies feed it — to win the round-500 longest-dragon tiebreak.

## What it computes / does
- Host form (yuna/tyr/Ares): from r250 relay the best crown known on alternating ray pairs; self-claim staggered by
  (id·7919) mod 120 when no fresh crown and L ≥ 3; challenger must be ≥3 longer; feeders from
  feed_from = 500 − feed_base − 0.6·(W+H) target the crown and step into a DEAD cell when its head is visible within 4.
  bifrost-v01: feed start 460 − ⌊0.6(W+H)⌋ (r400 Schooltime … r432 Devil/Portals/PD), production cutoff r380.
- ouroboros: crown from r200 (v02), small dragons die beside the crown (v09), relayed beacon with demotion and hand-off
  (v10). avery: v05 singular late crown (r300, len ≥ 8), v06 feeding from r410, v08 ouroboros envelope.
- Variants on clocks and eligibility: gavroche v25 crown_margin 3→1; spike x03 feed_range 40, x04 +feed_base 70, x05

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: in use — the relayed-beacon crown with a map-size feeding clock is in every Python host and in Ares;
  ouroboros v09/v10 and avery v06/v08 are the measured accepts (Python).
- Why it stopped: spike — "Conversion arms are paused until an audit of how well each dragon estimates the enemy's
  longest dragon exists"; chaewon — "Stop tuning conversion clocks (rejected on two hosts)"; yuna — "The local-crown
  remedy (x42/x43) did not change results; this is the top open problem" (maze maps: beacons stop at kelp and "every
  ≥3-long dragon self-claims a crown and nobody feeds"); vicious — "Better opening expansion and generally reliable crown
  concentration remain unresolved"; hunter-v23 — not stated.
- Host flag: all clock and eligibility variants were measured on Python hosts; the Ares crown code has not been varied
  in any lane (c1 notes: crown thresholds hard-coded).
- Revival condition: L15 (0.3) — "corpus: does crown survival/margin predict the win in the 28k set? if not → 0.15";
  C1-E: "winner's longest margin 0–1 in decided games". L33 makes crown location a special case of shared inclination.
- Map-identity note: the feeding clock keys on W+H (map dimensions), an observable but size-only signal.
