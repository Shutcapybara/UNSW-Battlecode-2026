# S-12 — Newborn siting

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L30 (named pay-down route) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Choose where the child is born — score the mid-game split by the food value near the child's spawn cell minus ally
crowding there — so newborns start near food and away from crowds.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: C1-C fix list item #2 (proposed on the cx chassis, never executed there); Sciel lane (GLM, R-2)
  sciel-01a-siting on Ares V06 atlas-off (sciel-00-base). Ares C++, production line.
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-cx-c01-eff.md` §fix list; `origin/r/sciel:docs/findings/2026-09-30-sciel-lane.md`;
  `origin/r/sciel:bots/sciel-01a-siting/params.hpp`; r3 finding recommendation 2.

## What it computes / does
- C1-C #2: "child's first bed chosen at birth for arrival-earliness"; acceptance −25 % Portals newborn AND pearls r100 up.
- sciel-01a: in `tyr_split_option`, add sit_weight × (best γ-discounted food value within sit_radius of the child spawn,
  from the parent's knowledge, − sit_reference) − ally-crowd penalty at spawn.
- Params (single setting, not swept): sit_radius 4, sit_nodes 40, sit_weight 0.8, sit_reference 5.0, sit_bed_horizon 10,
  sit_crowd_radius 2, sit_crowd_cost 0.5, sit_crowd_cap 2.0.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject (inert) on Ares C++ (production), 160-game seeded panel + gen panel.
- Why it stopped: "a len-4 parent just ate, so its tail is near food by construction" (sciel-status) — the siting term
  rarely changes the chosen split. Untested variant named by the author: hand the child its first target in the birth
  packet (new payload field).
- Revival condition: L30 — r3-03's own-body/newborn churn must be paid down "first (a siting rule for `ACT:tsplit`
  children)"; siting was tested on routine splits (tyr_split_option), not on `ACT:tsplit` escape children, so the
  L30 form remains untested (inferred from sciel-01a's definition). Newton's positional form (site by enemy-head
  distance) also unbuilt ([S-10](S-10.md)).
