# S-05 — Minimum-sacrifice and dead-end child search, feed-before-split

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** local hold · **Ledger:** L24, L30 (neither absorbs it) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

At a critical dead end, choose *which* part of the body to sacrifice: split off only two tail segments (keep the long
head), defer splits until the pocket's last pearl is eaten, then search child sizes largest→smallest for a tail child
with a free exit and forecast room.
