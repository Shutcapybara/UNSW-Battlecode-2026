# S-17 — One-ray portal probe and HOLD packet

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** reject · **Ledger:** L23, L06 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Spend a solo sonar ray through an adjacent portal whose landing is unseen (the echo says next turn whether a body stands
on the far line), and/or a HOLD packet: a dragon whose move leaves it beside a known paired portal with a blind landing
sends its post-move head cell + round; allies treat that landing as occupied for two rounds.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: reject on Ares C++ (production host, 160 games) and on sakura-s02; hold (n.s.) on chaewon; the only positive
  (ouroboros-s02) is a bundle whose gain the author attributes to the atlas.
- Why it stopped: Ares V07 — "targeted safety improvement did not preserve the full-panel result"; no combined/tuning
  run. Sakura — "The host's own portal_mode pricing already handles portals as well as the probe/HOLD/atlas stack ...
  the probe is one round stale exactly in the symmetric approach case"; s03 plan "drop the probe/HOLD stack unless a
  cheaper variant (probe only when the exit is pair-unknown) pays". Ouroboros-s02 — "The win comes from using portals
  more with the same per-step risk, not from making them safer"; "Portals dense 20-pair case ... probe and HOLD packet
  do not resolve". Cost noted: a probe turn suppresses all other rays (echo attribution).
- Revival condition: L23 (0.3) — "a cheaper trigger (only when an ally is within k of the pair) or stacking on a leak
  fix; revives if the head-on saving survives with economy flat". The head-on saving (−13.2 %) is the retained signal.

## Discrepancies
- Chaewon y04: director "+0.062 on 96 fixtures" vs chaewon branch "+0.036 on 196 fixtures" (different fixture sets).
- The HOLD packet is type 7 in chaewon-y05 but type 8 in Ares V07 (type 7 = Ares split handoff) (d2/d3 notes).
