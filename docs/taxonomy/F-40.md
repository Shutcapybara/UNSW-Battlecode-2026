# F-40 — Economy curve, map-normalised (econ~, p@k, references)

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use · **Ledger:** L21 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Pearls eaten by rounds 50/100/150/250, each divided by the field's median on the same map; `econ~` is the mean of the four checkpoint values and is the number the gates use.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: analysis instrument; consumed by the ra/renoir lane (`tools/ra/lane.py score`), Ares findings (v01, v05, v06, v07), sciel lane (`origin/r/sciel:tools/sciel/lane.py`), monoco (`origin/r/monoco:tools/ra/lane.py`), R-4 scorecard, esquie, lune (own `score.py`), RL-1 prompt.
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/BENCHMARKS.md` §Summary, §"The benchmark set"; `docs/analysis/benchmarks/map_reference_medians.json` (11 maps × 9 metrics); `tools/ra/README.md`; gen reference `gen_reference.json` (renoir-00's own per-map medians, frozen).

## What it computes
- Computation: per side-game pearls@k / field per-map median (e.g. pearls@100 medians: Autarky 96, Default 46, Devil 98, Portals 133.5, PD 60.5, PD10 58.5, QoS 42, Schooltime 51, Slithery 457, Trauma 34.5, Trophy 85.5); econ~ = mean over the four checkpoints of the per-checkpoint **median** across side-games (V06/R-4/renoir convention). Off-pool maps: normalised by renoir-00's own medians.
- CPU: offline (F1 extractor ≈ 1.1 s/game/core).
- Consumers: [A-27](A-27.md) evaluation protocol (the +0.05 gate, D-032 interval gate, D-036 local hold). No bot consumer.
- Evidence it matters (as a yardstick): map share of variance falls 0.74 → 0.13 (field) / 0.89 → 0.45 (zoo) when normalised; opponent share 0.10; seed ICC 0.77; log-odds per sd 1.04 zoo / 0.76 field. Zoo median 0.91–1.03 of field at every checkpoint; field winners 1.17–1.25; top-10 1.13–1.17. Resolution: pearls r50/100/150/250 need ~45/50/45/60 side-games to resolve half a between-bot sd.
- Flags: bed-capacity normalisation (`bed_yield`) rejected ("does not remove the map", map share 0.60). Seed noise exceeds the gate bar (renoir-00 econ~ 1.111 at seed 1 vs 1.182 at seed 2).
