# S-41 — Leader-coordinated fight protocol (S-3) — dropped without a bot

**Kind:** Solution · **Status:** dropped · **Ledger:** L09 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

A sonar protocol in which a nominated leader announces a fight and nearby allies converge on it — the ten-day plan's
S-3 — dropped after the corpus showed no coordination signature in the top ten.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: none built as S-3. Related earlier forms: fry-v04 captain/escorts (lowest-id captain, escorts trade enemy
  heads within 3 of the captain; never measured, archived "dominated by fry-v14"); hydra-v06 status sonar and
  2-slot sighting gossip (archived).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-28-director-decisions.md` D-030; `docs/analysis/C2-fight-anatomy.md`;
  `docs/hub/HYPOTHESES.md` L09; project `claude/ten-day-plan-2026-09-29.md` (S-3).

## What it computes / does
- Intended: a leader nominates a target over sonar; receivers commit and converge before contact.
- Test applied instead (C2-0, GLM 5.3): corpus fight detector over ranked games comparing top ten with the 55–85 band on
  movement-observable coordination (convergence, synced entries, pre-fight sonar), with a parity-at-contact
  decomposition of the initiator edge.

## Verdict and why it stopped
- Verdict: dropped (D-030, 29 Sep 12:55 UTC) — never built, never measured.
- Why it stopped: D-030 — "no movement-observable coordination signature in the top ten"; "The pooled +3.7 pp
  initiator edge is composition"; "nothing protocol-shaped is built without a payload-decoding study of teams that do
  send fight direction". C2: "S-3 verdict: a pricing rule, not a protocol". Fight cost went to R-3 and two local rules
  to R-2 ([S-40](S-40.md)).
- Revival condition: L09 (0.15) — "A payload-decoding study showing top teams send fight direction; or a top-ten
  replay set with synchronised entries above the band". L33 (0.4) keeps coordination alive as coupled beliefs, not a
  protocol; GLM's later note that other teams' payload semantics are "a dead end" is listed against L33.

## Discrepancies
- Payload decoding disagrees between readers: GLM reports payload vocabulary {3,4,5,6} for 306/470/62/7; Claude/main
  report near-uniform 64-bit payloads (306: 12k distinct values per game; 62: every ray payload zero) — likely a
  decoder field-offset difference (d4 Contradictions 1). The L09 trigger depends on which decoding is right.
