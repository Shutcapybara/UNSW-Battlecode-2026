- Crown (V06): from r250, claim if no fresh crown and rnd ≥ max(250, last_crown) + (me·7919 mod `crown_claim_spread` 120) and len ≥ `crown_claim_len` 3, or if longer by ≥ `crown_margin` 3; fresh for `crown_ttl` 20; crown lv 4.0, ignores exclusion.
- Feeding: feed_from = 500 − `feed_base` 40 − (W+H)·`feed_k` 0.6; feeders (fresh crown ≥ `feed_min_crown` 4, longer than us, within `feed_range` 16) target the crown and, when its head is visible within `feed_dist` 4, deliberately pick a move whose simulate status is DEAD; `w_flank` 3.0 keeps them off its flanks.
- Hunt: len ≤ `hunt_max_len` 5, r ≥ `hunt_from` 200, prey len ≥ `prey_min` 8: value `hunt_value` 1.0·min(prey_len, 40)·γ^steps.
- Varied (C++): hunt_from renoir-20a 80 (built, no row), 20b 40 (0.000, inert).
