# P-07 — Churn: pearls@k rewards corpse eating

**Kind:** Problem · **Status:** open · **Ledger:** L29 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

The economy yardstick (pearls eaten at r50/100/150/250) counts pearls from allied corpses, so a mechanism can raise "economy" by feeding the swarm to itself; the leak classes on Ares are partly the economy's own exhaust.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: Ares V06 and lanes (renoir, sciel, ouroboros-s02-econ), team-7 live compact wins, S1 swarms; field teams (corpse share 0.34–0.47).
- Code / docs: `docs/findings/2026-09-30-ra-lane.md` §P:churn; `docs/hub/HYPOTHESES.md` L29; `docs/findings/2026-09-28-analysis-claude-Q1-loss-anatomy.md` §5; `docs/public-replay-review-2026-09-25.md`.

## How it is measured
- No dedicated instrument delivered. Measured indirectly: share of pearls eaten that came from ally corpses (Renoir, vibing-recon), deaths/game by length, and the pairing of an economy rise ([F-40](F-40.md)) with ally head-on up / units@100 down ([F-42](F-42.md)).
- The corpse-share diagnostic assigned to R-4b (L29) is not found delivered.
- Three-number form: not given for any churn metric.
