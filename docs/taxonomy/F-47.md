# F-47 — Pace targets and live bed maps (C1-E)

**Kind:** Feature (analysis) · **Status:** in use (descriptive); no bot consumer of the C1-E files · **Ledger:** L17 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-30

Per-map × cohort pace, economy, survival and territory targets from ranked games, and bed cells with empirical spawn rates reconstructed from live replays.

## Where it lives
- Lineages: analysis (C1-E); precursor corpus pace curve used by pace-v01..v03 (`bots/pace-v03/params.py` L155-176).
- Code / docs: `docs/analysis/C1-pace-targets.md` (Readings 1–13); `game_stats/field_pace_targets.json`; `game_stats/live_beds.json`; `tools/analysis/c1e_beds.py`; `docs/analysis/C1-E-schooltime-spec.md`, `C1-E-qos-trauma-specs.md`; `claude/c1-e-status.md`.

## What it computes
- Pace targets: per map × cohort medians (units r25/50/100/200, total, longest, splits, first pearl, pearls r100, pearls/100dt, moves/pearl, deaths by class, territory, splits by window, newborn deaths/100, peak units) from 2,393 ranked games / 4,786 side-games; "Targets are per-map, never per-class" (director 29 Sep; class tables kept only as `_class_descriptive`).
- Live bed maps: bed cells from bed-origin spawn events over the same 2,393 games, with per-cell rates.
- Pace curve (precursor, 28 Sep): compact u25 6 / u50 9 / u100 18 / t250 80; open 6/11/19/100 (280 corpus games ≥ 1950).
- CPU: offline.
- Consumers: `field_pace_targets.json` and `live_beds.json` — no bot consumer found (grep; only C1-E docs/status and prompts reference them). The precursor curve was consumed by [A-06](A-06.md) through the pace controller (pace-v01..v03: split_val +1.5 per missing unit behind, −10 hold when ahead). Also [A-27](A-27.md) context for P entries.
- Evidence it matters: pace-v01 (curve consumer) −0.008 over 360 exact pairs (45/48, p 0.84); compact +0.049, open −0.046; v03 raised compact u100 13 → 17 and lost −0.055 (open −0.089) — "Production is pearl-bound, not decision-bound"; "a class-level target held the bot back on Schooltime". Descriptive: top 10 7/11/19/30 units at r25/50/100/200 vs band 6/9/15/23; team 7 − band −1 unit r100, −15 pearls by r100, −20 total r250.
- Flags: local map files differ from live beds — Schooltime 444 live vs 326 local (+36 %), Slithery 497 vs 339 (+47 %), QoS 472 vs 442, PD four layouts (76/88/76/88); atlas bed bitmaps (pace-v01-nolimit, chaewon) carry local counts. C1-E Schooltime spec step 2 requires live beds — not wired to any bot. F1 checkpoints include round-100 events vs by-r100 counters ("don't mix conventions").
