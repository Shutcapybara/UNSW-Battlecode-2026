What the z1 panel shows so far (seed 1, 560 games; the reading is ours, the figures below are the evidence)

  • The early game decides most games, and space is the best early measure. Controlling for both bots' zoo ratings, the strongest leading predictors of winning are units_share@100, territory@100 and total_share@100 (≈ +1.5 to +1.8 log-odds per within-map sd; within-map AUC 0.80–0.81). Next come bed-weighted territory, the share of the map visited and splits in rounds 0–100. Concentrating length early predicts losing: top1_share@100 has AUC 0.24.
  • The zoo is a monoculture in sonar and in production. Seven of eight bots send about 4 rays per dragon-turn in all four directions: exactly a quarter of rays are refracted, meaning one ray every turn is aimed into the bot's own neck. The eighth bot (ouroboros-m01) sends none. Every bot's median child is 2 segments long. Sonar features therefore identify bots (nearest-centroid 49% vs 12.5% chance) and tell us almost nothing about sonar strategy. This is the first place to look in the field corpus.
  • Deaths into kelp are a large, cheap loss. Wall deaths run at 8–9 per 1k dragon-turns for six bots (about a third of all deaths), and at 0 for kazuha-s01 and ouroboros-m01. Head-on collisions between allies add 1.5–4.8 per 1k.
  • Phases. The pooled HMM learns three states that match the anecdote:
    • opening: exploration +1 sd;
    • economy: spread out, concentration −0.5 sd;
    • crown: concentration +1.1 sd, production falling.
    The opening ends at a median of r15–r90 depending on the map. Caveat: the crown state also captures collapse. A losing side reduced to one dragon has high concentration, so losers "enter the crown" at a median of r185, against r435 for winners, and 52% of winners never enter it. Separating a deliberate crown race from attrition needs a growth signal on the longest dragon (in the backlog).
  • Validation that did not wait for live data.
    • All five V0 identities hold on 100% of side-games.
    • All four V1 probe checks pass.
    • EPG access correlates with the next 10 rounds' bed eats at r ≈ 0.66 within map (τ = 4 is best).
    • Territory predicts the next bed-pearl share at r ≈ 0.72; weighting by beds adds little over plain cells (0.71).
    • The death hazard falls about 7× from reach 3–5 (18.6 per 1k dragon-turns) to reach 46–61 (2.5 per 1k).
  • Seed stability (V2, 80 fixtures × 3 seeds). Most families have a median ICC of 0.8–0.96 (production 0.95, survival 0.96, economy 0.90). The leading r100 winners are noisier: territory@100 0.65 and units_share@100 0.67, so about a third of their variance is the seed. Concentration has the lowest family median (0.65).
  • hunter-v20 wins 12% here. It plays passively on open maps (on Queen of Spades: 1 pearl, no splits), which is consistent with its compact-map specialism.
