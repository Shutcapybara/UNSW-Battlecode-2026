Reading (4,563 field games, 143 teams, 28 Sep 10:15–20:43 UTC; zoo = z1 seed 1)

  • Raw pooled or hierarchical: the sampling barely moves game-level answers. Weighting teams equally instead of pooling games shifts the out-of-range share by a median of 1.7 percentage points (90th percentile 3.6, worst 6.6 on seen_share@499). Team identity explains a median of 19% of within-map variance. The hierarchy matters for a different question: whether we have the strategies at all. A median 25% of teams, and 79% of teams in the fighting family, sit outside the range our eight bots span.
  • The zoo is moderately unrepresentative. A median 19% of field side-games fall outside the zoo's per-map 5–95% range, against 10% if the zoo were representative. The figure is 18% for winners and 19% for the top-10 teams. The largest gaps:
    • Sonar. Field rays hit kelp far less often (47% of games below the zoo) and allies far more (44% above), refract into their own neck less, and use different compass mixes. The median field side sends about 4 IQR fewer rays per dragon-turn than the zoo median. The zoo's 5–95% range still covers the field only because ouroboros-m01 sends none.
    • Trading. Kills at r100–r250 are 23–32% above the zoo, and 82–85% of teams sit outside our bots' range. Most kills are mutual head-on collisions, both heads dying, so kill counts measure the volume of trades and are symmetric within a game (AUC 0.50). kill_length_ratio is the dominance measure.
    • Tempo. The first dragon of length 30 arrives earlier in the field (29% below the zoo, median shift −1.9 IQR) and the first dragon of length 20 is also earlier. The field keeps splitting in its crown phase (28% above).
    • Risk. The death rate while enclosed is lower in the field (23% below).
  • What decides games transfers. With the Elo expected score as a fixed offset, the top early predictors live are the ones the zoo found, with near-identical effects (log-odds per within-map sd, field vs zoo):
    • total_share@100: 1.55 vs 1.52;
    • territory@100: 1.63 vs 1.78;
    • units_share@100: 1.46 vs 1.58.
    Elo alone calls 65% of results. At team level, a higher rating goes with more dragons and more length at r100–r150 (Spearman ≈ 0.7) and with more trading.
  • Caveats.
    • Submission ids are absent from the corpus, so a team is pooled over whatever versions it ran during the day.
    • Ratings are the nearest earlier ladder snapshot; the snapshots start at 14:57, so earlier games use the first one.
    • Bed timings for server replays are recovered from maps/ by exact kelp match (120 of 120 checked).
    • The 10-dragon Prisoners Dilemma is kept out of the coverage tables because the zoo never plays it.
