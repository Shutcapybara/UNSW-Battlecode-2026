## Key findings
- Parameters are at a local optimum.
  - Single changes move results by ±3–5 per 100 games.
  - Arbitrary tie-break changes move them by ±10 per 108.
  - Test new versions on at least 150 games across original, transposed (T), flipped-X (FX) and flipped-Y (FY) maps.
- The real gains came from reading replays for structural leaks, not from tuning:
  - v06: dragons wavered between pearl beds that weren't due yet.
  - v07: portal dives were fatal about 37% of the time, often killing an ally too.
- Tried and rejected (e20–e26):
  - valuing fast pearl beds while out of sight
  - limiting dives by unit count
  - crowding weight, extra room near allies, blocking cells next to heads
  - pulling exploration toward the map centre
  - rotating tie-break order per dragon
  - rewarding moves that cut an enemy off
  - turning off hunting or strikes (worse)

## Weak spots
- **Devil:** 6–12. Enemy swarms take the fast-regrowing pearl beds in the centre by round 40.
- **Arena:** decided by rounds 20–30. Parameter changes there are noise.
- **Flipped maps (FX/FY):** we win about 50% against ouroboros-v13, versus about 75% on the originals. Ranked games use 5 random maps, so robustness matters.

## Promising next ideas
1. Work out the map's symmetry, so what we see on our side tells us the terrain, pearl beds and portal pairs on theirs.
2. Dedicated dragons that loop around the fast-regrowing (every-round) beds.
3. Protecting long dragons on big maps: about 9 per big_empty game are killed by short enemy heads.
4. Unfinished run (no conclusion): endgame settings `feed_base`, `lv_crown` and `crown_from`.

## Tooling
- **Runner:** `tools/sinbad/arena.py`
  - Results are cached by source hash; the device and cloud caches can be merged.
  - Test a parameter change with `--var name:k=v`.
  - Map sets: quick, quickT, quickFX, big, bigT; the FY maps are in `tools/ouroboros/holdout`.
- **Replay analysis:**
  - `tools/sinbad/deaths.py`: death causes; "trapped" means no free exit.
  - `tools/sinbad/eatmap.py`: where each team harvested bed pearls.
  - Traces: `touch /tmp/sinbad-trace-on`.
- **Working pattern:** edit `build/sinbad/dev`, freeze it with `snap.sh`, screen it with `arena.py`, then copy to `bots/sinbad-vNN` with a README and run a sandbox CPU check.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/sinbad-design.md`: framework.
  - `docs/sinbad-experiments.md`: full experiment log, ending with "State at wrap-up".
  - Project doc `claude/sinbad-status.md`.
- **Environment:**
  - The cloud container does about 3 games a minute.
  - The device VM swings between about 15 games a minute and unusable. Each call must finish a game within 160 seconds.
  - Never use `pkill -f` with a pattern that matches your own command line; it kills your own shell.
