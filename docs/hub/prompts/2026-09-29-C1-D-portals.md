# C1-D — portal discipline  (model: **GLM 5.3**)

Shared rules: `docs/hub/prompts/2026-09-29-C1-index.md`. Depends on C1-A's chassis. Bots `cx-d01-portals`,
`cx-d02-…`. Until the chassis lands, do §1 (the measurement) on existing replays — it stands on its own.

## 0. The job

Portals are killing us: every one of 1,005 JKS friendly head-on initiations in the audited games went through a
portal, and portal-step deaths are a tracked statistic nobody has yet driven down live (S2's H-portal-safety was never
confirmed). Make portal transit safe with hard rules first, measure, then relax.

## 1. Measure first (day 1, no chassis needed)

- From our own live replays (`HUB/replays/decoded/` via the hub mirror is not mounted for you; use
  `LIVE/state` and `public_replays/corpus/replays/` for team 7 games, and any local seeded fixtures of
  `bots/yuna-v03-core`) compute per game: portal steps taken, deaths within 2 rounds of a portal step by cause
  (wall, own body, ally body, head-on), deaths where two friendly heads used the same portal pair within 3 rounds,
  and the round distribution of those deaths. Per map, per side. Add it to `tools/replay_stats/` as
  `portal_deaths.py` if it is not already there (check `tools/game_stats.py` first: `portal_steps` exists).
- Do the same for three top teams from the corpus (306, 70, 801: how often do they transit, how often do they die
  doing it). That is the target.

## 2. The rules (on the chassis, in `params.hpp`-switchable form so each can be ablated)

- **Exit memory**: on entering a portal, record the exit edge and cell; a dragon never re-enters a pair whose exit
  cell was occupied or lethal on its last use within N rounds.
- **Probe before transit**: fire the sonar along the entry direction the turn before stepping in; the echo says
  whether the ray stopped on kelp, an ally or an enemy beyond the portal (echoes are counts by kind — read the docs'
  Sonar page). Transit only on a clean echo or a known-clear exit.
- **No double transit**: a per-team convention with no communication needed — a dragon may only enter a pair on
  rounds where `(round + dragon_id) % 2 == 0` for one direction and odd for the other; ablate against a
  sonar-message reservation (`SONAR` payload = pair id + round, read by whoever receives it).
- **Exit-cell simulation**: the chassis' exact one-step simulation extended through the portal: the step is legal
  only if the exit cell is free after every lower-id dragon has moved (id order is move order).
- Keep the gatherers' *willingness* to use portals: a rule that halves deaths by halving transits has not helped
  (sakura's regression was −16 net on portal-heavy maps from avoiding them).

## 3. Measure again

Same fixtures as the index; primary outcome portal-step deaths per game and per transit on Portals, Default,
Schooltime and Slithery Fight, in exact pairs against the chassis default; secondary: units r100 and pearls eaten by
r100 (transit must not cost pace). Ablate each rule separately, then all together.

Falsifiers: portal-step deaths fall by less than 25 % with all rules on; pearls eaten by r100 fall by more than 10 %
