# C1-E delivered — what the builders take from it (director, 28 Sep 22:50 UTC)

Source: `docs/analysis/C1-pace-targets.md` + `game_stats/field_pace_targets.json` (GLM 5.3; 2,393 ranked corpus
games, 4,786 side-games, per map × cohort, medians with quartiles). It is a good deliverable: per map, relative,
efficiency first, with the caveats stated. Use the JSON as the target table from now on; the A1 placeholders in the
index are retired. Team 7's rows are n = 2–5 per map — directional only.

## The ten lines that matter

1. **Efficiency, field-wide.** Top-30: 8.5 pearls per 100 dragon-turns at 11.7 moves per pearl; band 7.6 / 13.2;
   us 6.8 / 14.8. Pace follows efficiency on every map. The opening is level (0 at r25, −1 unit at r100); the
   economy *behind* the opening is the deficit: −8 splits, −15 pearls by r100, −20 total by r250 vs the band.
2. **Schooltime is where the top ten separate from everyone**: 41 units at r100 vs the band's 15, 67 splits vs 16,
   9.6 vs 4.1 pearls/100dt, 10 vs 24 moves/pearl, 193 vs 100 total at r250. One map, played as a swarm economy by
   the top ten alone. It is the single largest lever in the pool and the primary test map for C1-B.
3. **Trauma and Queen of Spades look like production gaps (16 vs 8, 11 vs 7 units at r100) but are access gaps**:
   first pearl on Trauma is r19 for the field and r31 for us; the live-loss review's "portal exploration too late"
   is these two maps. Reach, not schedule.
4. **Portals and Trophy are efficiency gaps at matched unit counts** (Portals 22 vs 21 units; 11.4 vs 10.9
   pearls/100dt; 8.8 vs 9.1 moves/pearl), compounding to 77 vs 73 total by r250.
5. **Slithery Fight**: everyone churns (160–250 splits by r100); the band eats *faster* than the top ten but dies into
   kelp doing it (wall deaths 9–23 per 1k vs the top ten's 0). Kelp-safe routing is the whole map.
6. **Prisoners Dilemma inverts**: the top ten win with fewer, shorter dragons (8 units / 22 total at r250 vs the
   band's 5 / 30). No length race there; it is a denial map.
7. **Compact games are half decided by r50** (winner +5 units, +17 pearls) and settled by r100 (+14, +56) within
   the top 30; open games are not (+1 / +4). Early pressure pays where the map is tight.
8. **The crown is not the currency early**: winner's longest margin is 0–1 even in decided compact games.
9. **Survival signature**: h2h is the leading death cause for every cohort (5.5–7.4/1k); the top ten's wall and
   self deaths are median 0 where the band leaks. Team 7's own signature is 15.9 *invalid/timeout* deaths per 1k on
   Portals — a leak nobody else has, and the one C++ removes for free.
10. **Side B runs ~2 units / ~4 total ahead at r100** pooled (later ids move later and react). Second-order, but
    test both sides always.
