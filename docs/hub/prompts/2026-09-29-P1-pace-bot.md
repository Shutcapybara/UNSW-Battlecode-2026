## 0. The question, in one paragraph

Every live source we have fielded loses the same way: behind on total length by round 100 in 85–95 % of losses,
eliminated on compact maps at a median round of 122–158, and the r100 lead predicts the outcome at 0.78 vs 0.14
(`docs/findings/2026-09-28-analysis-claude-Q1-loss-anatomy.md`). The live opponents field 17–24 units at r100 and
52–103 total length at r250 against us; our lines field 9–14 and 27–51. **Build a bot whose only objective is to
reproduce the field's material curve** — units at r25/r50/r100, total at r250 — on the ten public maps, by whatever
opening means work, subject only to not dying for it. Then measure what hitting the pace changes. This is a probe of
a hypothesis, not a product: if pace alone moves the paired win share against band-class opponents, the S2 line
knows where to spend; if it does not, the field's advantage is something else and we stop chasing production.

## 3. What to build

Start from a host inside the band: `bots/fenrir-v18-arrival-ready-beds` (byte-identical to the live control 9508;
teammate code — copy with attribution, do not edit in place), `bots/yuna-v05-core`, or `bots/chaewon-y04-probe`
(yuna-v05 + atlas + newborn-neck fix + portal probe; +0.062 on 96 fixtures vs yuna-v05). Do **not** start from
ouroboros-v10-beacon (0.15–0.30 against the local band; three S1 lineages built on it and all regressed).

The pace controller is the only new policy: at each turn a dragon compares the team's material (units, total — it
knows `UNIT_COUNT` from the header and its own length; sonar beacons or the birth certificate carry the rest) against
`pace_target[stage]` and shifts the production/foraging balance to close the gap: earlier and smaller splits when
behind on units (child length 2, `split_min_len` 4, salvage split when boxed), bed pre-positioning and confirmed-pearl
sprints when behind on total, the host's default when on pace. Hard constraints that the controller may not violate:
no certain-death step (exact 1–3 step simulation), no split without a free child exit, newborn deaths within 10
rounds ≤ 10 per 100 births (measure it), self+wall deaths ≤ 5 per 1k dragon-turns. Fix the newborn neck bug if your
host has it (chaewon's finding: a split child keeps the parent's facings; link the neck by adjacency). Everything else
stays the host's. Emit `LOG ACT:pace+` when the controller pushes production, `ACT:pace-` when it holds back, so the
activation contract can see it fire.

Three arms on the same seeded fixtures: **host (control)**, **pace** (controller on), **pace-nolimit** (controller on,
survival constraints off — to measure what the constraints cost). Optional fourth arm if time allows: pace with the
atlas (chaewon's `tools/chaewon/build_atlas.py`).

## 4. Measurement

- Panel (S1 §7.1 form): ten live maps × both sides × seeds 1–3 × opponents. **Opponent set changes from S1:**
  `bots/fenrir-v18-arrival-ready-beds`, `bots/sinbad-v07-divecap`, `bots/gavroche-v32-supported-divecap`,
  `bots/ouroboros-m01-vibing-mimic`, `bots/yuna-v05-core`, `bots/chaewon-y04-probe`, and any clone of 62/545 that
  exists by then (`bots/clone-*`). Pair by (map, side, seed, opponent). Report score, better/worse pairs, sign-test p,
  per-map-class deltas — never a rating.
- **Pace attainment**: per arm and map class, the median and q25 of units r25/r50/r100 and total r250 against the
  target; the share of games on pace at r100; and the survival curve (share of games with units > 0 by stage).
- **What moves with pace**: over every game of the panel (all arms, all opponents, both sides — the unit is the
  game), fit win ~ stage statistics (units r25/r50/r100, total r250, longest r400, deaths per 1k by cause, splits,
  newborn deaths, portal steps, sonar rays) with a simple regularised logistic model and report the coefficients and
  the leave-one-map-out AUC. Say which two statistics carry the prediction. This is the local half of the A2 question.
- Metered probes (S1 §7.2) on both toolkits, now four fixtures: Schooltime A, Portals B, **Slithery Fight A, Trauma
  B**, vs `bots/sinbad-v07-divecap`. Gate: zero faults, max < 80 M, p99 < 60 M.
- Activation contract in `CANDIDATE.toml`: `markers = [["ACT:pace+", 0, 100, 10], ["ACT:pace-", 0, 500, 1]]` plus the
  host's own if it has any.
