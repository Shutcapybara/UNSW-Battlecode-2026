# tew-v17-balanced-compact-growth

Parent: `tew-v13-compact-survival`. Keeps Tew's modular state, communication, evaluator, safety veto and compact production ladder.

Hypothesis: v13 scored 12–6 against recent external lines but lost 2–4 to v12. Replay statistics show v13's compact production peak was notably below the parent in their Arena losses. Keep v13's higher compact attack and split thresholds, but restore the small-map team target from 20 to 26 to recover production without reverting the more selective tactics.

This is a focused native experiment against v12, v13, Avery v05 and Drake v03 on Arena, Stronghold and default. Native games do not certify judge-budget safety.

## Results

Native focused comparison `experiment_data/tew-v17-balanced-compact-growth_20260925074620547319`: **15–9**, no game errors or runtime faults. It scored 2–4 vs v12, 3–3 vs v13, 4–2 vs Avery v05 and 6–0 vs Drake v03. Map totals: 5–3 Arena, 6–2 Stronghold and 4–4 default. Against the same three external opponents as v13, the record was also 12–6. Restoring target 26 tied v13 directly without a measured aggregate gain.
