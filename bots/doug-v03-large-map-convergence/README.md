# Doug V03 — large-map convergence

**Base:** Tyr V12, including its Devil scouting tie-break and reduced search
caps.

**Changes:** Doug preserves Tyr V12's original timing on compact maps. On
boards at least 1,100 tiles, production stops at round 290, the length-value
ramp starts at round 290, crown claims start at round 200 and require length
7, and feeding opens earlier. This is an adaptive phase change for the larger
dragons the request targets; Devil, Queen of Spades, and other compact boards
retain Tyr's population-first behavior.

This is an early-convergence experiment, not a promotion claim. Results are
recorded with the Doug comparison panels as they are run.
