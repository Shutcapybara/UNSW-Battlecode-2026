# yeji-s02-mapfield

**Lineage:** Yeji. **Parent:** `yeji-s01-swarm-dissolve` (crown/dissolve layer reset to the host, as in `yeji-s01p-production`). **Status:** frozen; **not deployable** — its map-prior loader exceeds the CPU cap on Schooltime's first turn in the judge sandbox (all three dragons die in round 0). Fixed in `yeji-s03-compactfield`.

Adds:
- **Map prior** (`mapprior.py`, generated by `tools/yeji/build_prior.py` from the public `maps/*.map`): a dragon recognises its map from its first 7×7 view (size + every known edge must match exactly, one candidate only) and loads the whole terrain (kelp, portal pairs) and the fast pearl beds (max gap ≤ 300). Unwatched fast beds get target value `w_bed_prior × P(pearl)`; waypoints get `w_zone_bed × sqrt(zone rate)`.
- **Bed field** `w_field = 12`: move value × (field(after) − field(now)), field = Σ rate·exp(−pathdist/6) normalised to 1.
- **Portal-exit memory** `w_blind_occ`: an unseen portal landing costs P(occupied) × (value + 2), P from bodies seen there in the last 4 rounds and fresh ally reports beside it.
- **Direct certificate rays** `cert_direct`: the certificate also goes on any straight ray that stops on the child (delivery 0.37 → 0.57).
- Unused (default 0, screened): `diss_any` (Vibing++-style dissolve beside any longer ally head), `w_parity` (Lanchester trade pricing), `unit_cells`, `target_rate_d0`.

Panel (160 paired fixtures, unswbc 1.2.2, seed 1): **0.519** (control v10 0.494: +0.025, 22/18; vs `yeji-s01p-production` −0.013, 22/24). By map vs P: **devil +0.44**, slithery +0.19, trauma +0.12, dilemma +0.06 — but **default −0.50**, QoS −0.19, trophy −0.12. Compact maps: units r100 26 vs opponents 13, longest r499 32 vs 19. Open maps: units r250 16 vs 32 — the field pulls everyone onto the same beds and the open-map economy stalls (default: pearls 193 vs 405 for P on the same fixture). `@dissolve_on=1` (the S1 dissolve on top): 0.469 (−0.05 vs s02). Screens on 32 compact fixtures vs s02: `w_parity=1` −0.03 (0/1), `unit_cells=16` −0.09 (2/5), `w_field=20` −0.03 (3/4).

Metered: Portals as B p99 56.9M, **max 85.8M** (over the 80M local gate); Schooltime as A: CPU exceeded on turn 0.

> **Out-of-sample caveat (added 29 Sep).** The map prior only fires on the ten public maps it was built from; tournament maps are out of sample, so on them this bot plays without it. The in-sample gains above are not evidence of tournament strength. Held-out evaluation: see `yeji-s06-onlinebeds`.
