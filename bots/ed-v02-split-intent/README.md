# Ed v02: split-intent hint

- **Lineage:** Ed, created for the 2026 temporal-policy campaign.
- **Parent:** `ed-v00-serre-control` (frozen Serre v01 source).
- **Change:** a forager sends its current target down the body ray on a normal
  split. A child that hears a fresh packet softly discounts pearls and other
  targets within three toroidal steps of that location for eight turns starting when the child hears it.
  The hint does not alter crown handoff packets, and newborns retain all local
  choices when no better destination exists.
- **Hypothesis:** a child will leave the parent's high-density pocket instead
  of turning back into a tight space or dead end.
- **Active parameters:** `fork_intent=1`, `fork_age=8`, `fork_radius=3`,
  `fork_value_scale=0.35`.
