# Doug V02 — deeper early convergence

**Base:** Tyr V12, including its Devil scouting tie-break and reduced search
caps.

**Changes:** V02 makes the phase shift decisive: the production split window
and non-crown growth ramp move to round 260, crown claims open at round 180,
and claims require an 8-segment dragon. Feeding also opens earlier and the
late length value is stronger, so the team should bank material into fewer,
larger dragons before the round-500 tiebreak.

This is an early-convergence experiment, not a promotion claim. Results are
recorded with the Doug comparison panels as they are run. V02 is intentionally
more aggressive than V01; if it gives up the early population race, V03 will
restore production while retaining the measured crown timing.
