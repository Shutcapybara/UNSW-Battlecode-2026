# Sparta 05 — queen length reserve

Sparta 05 forks Sparta 04. Its original queen may use an ordinary or opening
split only when the resulting parent has length seven or more. This retains
the queen's length through early growth while allowing production after she
has grown enough. The emergency escape split remains unchanged. Other dragons
keep Sparta 01's production and the team keeps Sparta 01's enemy-queen hunt.

Sparta 05 scored 13–11 against Sparta 01 and 7–17 against Carthage 05. It made
27 queen split actions (15 in Sparta 04), killed the enemy queen in 14 games
(13 in Sparta 04), and kept the queen alive@490 in 3/32 reached games. The
seven-segment reserve changed these counts slightly but did not alter wins or
queen survival. See the
[length-reserve finding](../../docs/findings/2026-10-02-sparta05-queen-length-reserve.md).
