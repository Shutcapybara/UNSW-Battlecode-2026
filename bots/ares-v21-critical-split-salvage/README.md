# Ares V21 — critical split salvage

V21 starts directly from Ares V19. It keeps V19's five-step, body-blocked reach probe and critical enclosure trigger. When the best move remains in the critical reach band, V21 also replaces a smaller routine split with the existing tail escape split when that split is legal. The escape split moves the largest viable tail section into a child and leaves two segments at the trapped head end, giving that parent the shortest body to turn back out.

This targets the live-loss review's repeated dead-end feeding cases, where the split size left too many segments in the trapped section. It does not change routine splits outside the critical enclosure condition. If no safe tail exit exists, V21 keeps the selected action.

Development screen: all 10 maps and both seats, seed 1 versus Ares V19: 9-11 with zero runner errors. A separate all-map V09 screen was 10-10. This split-size upgrade did not beat V19.
