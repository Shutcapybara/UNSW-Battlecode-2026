# Tyr V09 — Devil centre scout

**Base:** tyr-v01-yuna-momentum.

**Changes:** During the first 42 rounds on the 32x16 Devil map, foragers receive a
score bonus for advancing toward the central columns. Candidate endpoints also
keep a small buffer around visible friendly body trails to reduce lane cutoffs.

This is an experimental map-focused arm. Results and any further changes are
recorded in docs/tyr-family.md after evaluation.
