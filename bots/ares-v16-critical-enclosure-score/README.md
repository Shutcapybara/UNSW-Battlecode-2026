# Ares V16 — critical enclosure score

V16 carries forward V15's five-step, body-blocked reach probe and its 0.35 score weight. It applies the reach-delta score only when current reach is at most eight cells. The eight-cell gate is intended to focus the same intervention on genuinely tight pockets; the benchmark's enclosed definition remains reach5 <= 15.

V15's all-10-map, both-seat seed-1 screen against V09 reduced pooled `death_rate_enclosed_per1k` from 82.95 to 71.00, and mean enclosed share from 0.232 to 0.125. It lost 6-14, with mean r100 pearls falling from 134.0 to 74.4, r100 units from 23.2 to 14.8, and wall deaths from 9.66 to 7.40 per 1k dragon-turns. That result rejects the broad activation condition.
