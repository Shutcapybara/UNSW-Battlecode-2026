# ein-dog-x05-handoff: crown tail-transfer under pressure

Parent: ein-dog-v01-control. One gated edit in `split_option`, default off:

- `crown_handoff` (set 1): from `handoff_from` (380), the crown may
  `SPLIT L-2` -- the long body becomes the rear child, spawned at the old
  tail facing away; the parent keeps 2 segments. The chassis's existing
  handoff sonar packet tells the child it is the crown (roles.INHERIT), and
  crown beacons demote the old parent within a couple of rounds. The split
  carries `handoff_bonus` (+6) so it wins exactly when ordinary moves are
  priced badly (boxed or threatened crown); it conserves total length.

Source evidence: longhubao (team 470, rank 1) performs this transfer in
99.7% of non-length-4 splits at r400-499 (SPLIT 73 from length 75); our
round-limit losses show crowns losing length races while boxed or traded.

Off-state (`crown_handoff` 0) is behaviorally identical to the control.
