# Tyr V12 — Devil scouting tie-break

**Base:** Tyr V01 with Devil-only opening changes.

**Changes:** For the first 42 rounds on the 32x16 Devil map, foragers score
moves toward its two central columns. A small positive-only ID lane bonus
diversifies scouting routes for the first 100 rounds without penalizing a route
toward a valuable resource. Friendly endpoints keep a two-tile buffer from
visible friendly trails. Target, sprint, and flood caps are reduced from V01.

**Result:** Beat Tyr V01 **12–0** across six fixed seeds with both seats in
native mode. The seed-1 judge-sandbox smoke also won in both seats. Sandbox CPU
overruns still occurred and are recorded in the Tyr family notes; the native
panel had no runtime-fault markers.
A separate native panel also beat the documented Yuna V05 Core finalist
12–0 on Devil across six seeds and both seats.

This is a Devil-specific experimental branch, not an all-map promotion. See
[the Tyr family notes](../../docs/tyr-family.md).
