# Tyr V30 — stronger early split production

**Base:** Tyr V25 pearl-funded sprint defense.

**Change:** Increase early split-production action value from 8 to 10, continue
it through round 40 instead of 30, and allow production to 40 rather than 32
units. This targets the early unit and pearl deficit seen in paired Dilemma and
Trophy replays. V25's safety and all other V12 settings remain in place.

**Status:** Experimental. Paired seeded comparison against V12 is pending.
