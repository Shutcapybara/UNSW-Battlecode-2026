# Doug V16 — early large-map feeding

**Base:** Tyr V12, including its Devil scouting tie-break and reduced search
caps.

**Changes:** Doug preserves Tyr V12's production and growth timing everywhere.
On boards at least 1,100 tiles, crown claims start at round 200 and require a
length-6 dragon. Large-map feeding opens around round 250, reaches feeders from
30 tiles, and commits at a three-tile approach distance so the crown can bank
length before the final rounds. Compact boards keep Tyr's original feeding.
During rounds 20–180 on large boards, sequential engine IDs provide a cautious
population frontier; equal-length trades gain value only when Doug estimates
its own living population is ahead. The signal expires when stale.

This is an early-convergence experiment, not a promotion claim. Results are
recorded with the Doug comparison panels as they are run.
