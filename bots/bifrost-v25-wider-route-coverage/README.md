# Bifröst V25 — wider compact-map route coverage

V25 branches from V23's pearl-and-bed route ownership rule. It doubles each
nearby ally's bounded route search from 64 to 128 discovered cells, while
keeping the three-ally limit, compact-map gate, two-step detour threshold, and
V01 scoring for exploration and territory. The hypothesis is that V23's route
search often falls back to torus distance before reaching a resource target.

The native screen used the six-map, four-opponent Bifröst focus panel. V25
scored 41–19 overall (7–5 against V01) and 34–14 on the 48 external
fixtures, with one paired gain and ten regressions versus V01’s 43–5. Devil
regressed to the control’s 5–3; Trophy stayed even at 7–1. The larger route
cap is rejected. See the [family notes](../../docs/bifrost-family.md).
