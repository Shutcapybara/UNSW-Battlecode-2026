# Bifröst V26 — tuned resource-route ownership

V26 starts from the complete Bifröst V01 source and retains all 13 V01
overrides. It adds the V23 route-aware teammate ownership rule for known
pearls and beds on maps up to 625 cells, using up to three nearby allied heads,
a 64-cell BFS cap, and a two-cell minimum route/torus distance difference.
Exploration and territory keep V01 scoring. This is the first route candidate
in this series that preserves the full V01 tuning profile.

The focused screen completed without errors at 46–14 overall (7–5 against
V01). On the 48 external fixtures it scored 39–9 against V01's 43–5, with
one gain and five regressions. Dilemma fell to 4–4 and Devil to 4–4, while
Autarky, Default, Queen of Spades, and Trophy matched the V01 control. Route
ownership is rejected with the full V01 tuning. See the [family notes]
(../../docs/bifrost-family.md).
