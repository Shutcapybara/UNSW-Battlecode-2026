Exact executable copy of aramis-x09-frontier, selected on the frozen frontier screen.

Direction comes from the swarm gradient, not team or spawn side. Tracing disabled.

Research release: see [the frontier report](../../docs/aramis-frontier.md) for
matched density controls, reserve regressions and judge coverage. The frontier
screen improved from 11–13 to 14–10, but the fresh reserve regressed from the
density control's 7–5 to 5–7 (Monte Christo v01: 9–3). No promotion is supported;
Monte Christo v01 remains the playing baseline. Six source-identical judge games
had zero timeouts, peaking at 93.85M CPU points and 22,609,920 bytes.
