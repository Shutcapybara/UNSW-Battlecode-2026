# Sparta 06 — five segment queen reserve

Sparta 06 forks Sparta 05. Its original queen may use an ordinary or opening
split only when the resulting parent has length five or more. This retains
the queen's length through early growth while allowing production sooner than
Sparta 05. The emergency escape split remains unchanged. Other dragons keep
Sparta 01's production and the team keeps Sparta 01's enemy-queen hunt.

Sparta 06 scored 13–11 against Sparta 01 and 6–18 against Carthage 05. It
recorded 31 queen splits and 15 enemy-queen kills (up from 27 and 14 in Sparta
05), but only 2/32 queens were alive at round 490, compared with 3/32 in
Sparta 05. The lower reserve does not improve queen safety and loses one more
game to Carthage 05. See the
[five-segment reserve finding](../../docs/findings/2026-10-02-sparta06-queen-reserve-five.md).
