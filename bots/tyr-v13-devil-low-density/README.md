# Tyr V13 — Devil low-density search

**Base:** Tyr V12 Devil scout tie-break.

**Changes:** Limits retained density reports to 12, sends one density ray with
no relay, and lowers target, sprint, and flood caps to reduce rare judge CPU
overruns. Keeps V12's center push, positive-only scouting-band tie-break, and
friendly body clearance.

V12 won the first sandbox Devil match and reduced friendly-body deaths to 21,
but had one CPU overrun. V13 is the CPU-headroom follow-up.
