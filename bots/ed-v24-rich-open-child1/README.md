- **Lineage:** Ed, directly descended from the v02 split-intent candidate and
  frozen v00 foundation.
- **Change:** use a one-segment child only during the first 80 global rounds,
  while the team has fewer than 12 units, and when the prospective launch cell
  has at least ten confirmed reachable cells and three current pearls within
  five known-terrain steps.

- **Reason:** prior size-three time/state variants changed production but did
  not improve wins. This tests smaller, faster early expansion only where the
  newborn has a verified resource opportunity and enough room to escape.
- **Feature switch:** child1_rich_opening=0 restores v02 split size.
