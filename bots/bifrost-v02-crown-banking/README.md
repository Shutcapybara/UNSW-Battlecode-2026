# Bifröst v02 — crown banking

This variant retains Bifröst v01's Gavroche V54 base and portal-exit memory.
It tests Avery v08's measured crown-race schedule: earlier eligible crown
claims, a minimum crown length of five, and a broader late feeding radius for
small dragons. Feeding is delayed until at least round 400, preserving
Gavroche's later map-size timing on compact boards. Dragons longer than 20 do
not switch to feeder role.

The hypothesis comes from v01's Big Empty losses: all 32 ended at round 500,
and the candidate often had higher total length or pearl count but a shorter
longest dragon. Avery v08 documented Big Empty improving from 7-5 to 9-3 as
crown lengths rose from 45 to 55. Those results came from a different gauntlet;
this variant needs its own paired benchmark against v01.
Family-level matchup observations and iteration status are tracked in the [Bifröst family notes](../../docs/bifrost-family.md).
