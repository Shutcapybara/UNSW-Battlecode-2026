# Bifröst v05 — population-aware production

This experiment retains Bifröst v01's movement, portal-memory and endgame policy. It makes ordinary production splits decrease in value as the team approaches a size target (26 units on compact maps, 40 on medium maps, 60 on large maps) and suppresses them when more than ten allied body segments are visible. The target range follows the measured Godel family approach; the local crowd gate targets the reported Queen of Spades portal congestion.

The screen includes Autarky, Prisoner's Dilemma, Default, Devil, Queen of Spades, and Trophy. See the [family notes](../../docs/bifrost-family.md) for all reported issues and results.


## Test result

Rejected after a 60-game focused screen: 36 wins and 24 losses, with no errors or runtime faults. It lost 3–9 to Bifröst v01. On 48 external fixtures paired by opponent, map, side and seed, v01 scored 43–5 and v05 scored 33–15; v05 gained one result and regressed on 11. The team-wide population cap is not retained. See the [candidate report](../../experiment_data/bifrost-v05-population-aware-production_20260927122158858320/summary.md) and [matched V01 control](../../experiment_data/bifrost-v01-portal-memory_20260927121857063579/summary.md).
