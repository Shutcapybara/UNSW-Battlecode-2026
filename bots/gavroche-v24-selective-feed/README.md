# Gavroche v24: selective sprint and late feeding

Parent: Gavroche v23. Keeps three-step moves for length-4–7 hunters; disables
them for lengths 8–11. Adds a distinct, guarded endgame donation policy based
on Avery v06: only donors of length 10 or less, beginning at round 410 on
64x64, and only die in place within two tiles of a fresh, visible, longer
crown. The dying donor sends no sonar. This tests whether controlled pearl
banking improves the longest-dragon finish without harming the early economy.
