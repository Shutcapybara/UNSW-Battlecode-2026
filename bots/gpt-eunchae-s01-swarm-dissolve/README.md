# gpt-eunchae-s01-swarm-dissolve

This is an initial parameter and policy iteration on `ouroboros-v10-beacon`. It raises early production targets to 64, keeps the host's exact split-exit safety checks, elects a crown from round 250, and applies a 300/400-round onset based on the public pool maps' dimensions. Small donors only dissolve adjacent to a directly observed crown; the crown must be longer. The existing backward-ray handoff is retained.

## Measurement report

**Strategy:** λ-unit proxy is increased through round 100 by raising team targets to 64; λ-length remains the inherited foraging value; crown selection starts at round 250; control remains unweighted. Dissolution onset is round 300 on Portals and Slithery Fight dimensions and round 400 otherwise. Conversion funnel and panel outcomes have not been measured.

**Execution:** Production and host salvage are enabled with two-segment production children, exact host legality checks, and round-100 production cutoff. Crown broadcast and direct-crown escort/dissolve are enabled. Strike remains inherited/off by default. The production/salvage/dissolve ablations and activation counts remain to be run.

**Implementation:** Installed toolkit is unswbc 1.0.0. Schooltime as A vs sinbad-v07: max 42.4M, p99 34.2M, 1,613 own turns. Portals as B vs sinbad-v07: max 43.2M, p99 34.8M, 5,371 own turns. No faults or CPU-limit messages appeared in either verbose log. Both activation contracts appeared in the Portals run: 56 prod, 102 salv, 116 crown, 96 escort, 4 dissolve, and 59 cert markers. This verifies marker emission, not the full replay-derived mechanism funnel. A temporary pip install for unswbc 1.2.2 returned “No matching distribution found”; the available 1.0.0 runner has no `--seed` option. The paired seeded panel could not be run. No degradation-mode sweep was run.

**State:** Host terrain, pearl, threat, ally and crown memories remain. Consumer is the inherited route/evaluation, risk model, crown selection, feeding, and portal routing. No dedicated scout or density gossip was added.

**Messaging:** Existing checksum tagged self, enemy, portal, bed, doom, crown, and handoff packets remain. The handoff travels on the backward ray and is treated as a certificate marker when a new process receives it. This packet does not carry every field in the proposed S1 certificate layout. Send counts and certificate ablation have not been measured.

**Momentum:** Inherited target selection and process memory are retained; no explicit target-switch margin or new hysteresis was added. Newborn-death and first-pearl ablations have not been measured.
